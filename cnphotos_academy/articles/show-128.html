Touch the Beauty of the Soul: Feel the Power of Black-and-White Portraits

Published: 2023-04-12

Source link: https://www.cnphotos.net/academy/show-128.html

Settings and environments may look alike, and the emotion of a portrait changes from photographer to photographer. But a black-and-white portrait makes the feeling and mood of the portrait stronger.

'When you photograph people in color, you photograph their appearance. But when you photograph them in black and white, you photograph their souls.' - Ted Grant

Today we put together a set of black-and-white portraits, so you can feel their power.
