Leica Camera2024/2025sets new records in fiscal year

Published: 2025-09-30

Strong global demand for cameras and innovative products drives sales growth in the core photography business and new fields

2025930，Mainland China. In the just-ended 2024/2025 fiscal year, the Leica Camera Group set a new record.2025. In this year the company marks the Ith anniversary of the Leica I camera.100. For the fourth straight year it set new records for revenue and profit. From 20244 to 20253, the premium optics maker Leica posted revenue growth year over year of 7.6%，, reaching about 5.96 million euros (5.54 million euros the prior year). This growth comes mainly from the strong core photography and mobile (smartphone) businesses. At the same time, premium home-theater projectors (smart projection), accessories (watches), and premium eyeglass lenses (Leica Vision) all showed good momentum, lifting the Group overall revenue. In a challenging market, sports optics stayed solid and remains Leica second-largest business.

The Group ongoing success confirms its forward-looking strategy. The company focuses on strengthening core products in optics and mechanics, and through its unique design and operation, it gives users an unmatched product experience.

Matthias Harsch, CEO of Leica Camera AG (Matthias Harsch）) said: With our premium Made in Germany products, we keep meeting the rising global demand for cameras. In the past fiscal year we launched new classic camera models and released several products, including the LeicaLUX app and the LeicaCine Play 1 mini projector. Leica strong results rest on steady investment in innovation and the steady expansion of our global sales network."


All business regions grew again

The Leica Camera Group posted broad sales growth across all regions in 2024/2025. Europe grew the most, with revenue up year over year 7.6%，, driven by a strong gain of 11.4% in Germany. Asia (+7.3%）) and North America (+6.2%）) also grew steadily. To broaden its international sales and strengthen key strategic markets, the Group opened new Leica Stores in Wuhan (China), Tokyo (Japan), and Seoul (South Korea). Online business also stayed strong, contributing 12.5% percent growth. Looking ahead, Leica will keep expanding its global retail network and, through a multi-channel model of physical retail, auctions, and e-commerce, further strengthen its unique market position.

Success factors and new products in photography

In the past fiscal year Leica launched several new models that drove sales growth. As one of the few brands still making film cameras, Leica also does well in digital. The compact full-frame LeicaQ3 is a top seller. In 20249 it added a version with a 43 mm fixed lens. The LeicaSL system SL3 and SL3-S models are also popular. The LeicaD-Lux 8 adds a small compact option. In the M system, Leica launched a special version of the M11-D—— classic full-frame digital camera, without a digital display, continuing the pure photography idea.

Leica at 100: a witness of the century

For Leica Camera AG, 2025 marks 100 years since the Leica I. In I 100 1925In I the Leica I debuted at the Leipzig Spring Fair. It was the first mass-produced Leica 35mm camera. Compact and portable, it created a new way to photograph and changed photography. Under the slogan Leica at 100: a witness of the century, Leica Camera AG will hold events in cities such as Dubai (UAE), Milan (Italy), New York (USA), Shanghai (China), and Tokyo (Japan). Leica Camera——A companion of photography

Leica Camera AG (Leica Camera AG）) is an international company that makes premium cameras, lenses, and sports optics, with more than 150 years of history. As part of its growth strategy, it has expanded into mobile imaging (smartphones), high-quality eyeglass lenses, and watches, and holds a place in home theater with its own projectors.

Leica Camera AG is headquartered in Wetzlar, Germany, with another site in Famalicao, Portugal. It owns a global sales network of more than 120 Leica Stores.

The Leica brand stands for excellence, German craftsmanship, industrial design, and innovation. As part of its brand culture, Leica promotes image culture and runs about 30 Leica Galleries worldwide, the Leica Academy, and international awards such as the Leica Hall of Fame (Leica Hall of Fame Award） and the Leica Oskar Barnack Award (Leica Oskar Barnack Award, LOBA）。
