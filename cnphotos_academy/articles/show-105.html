How to Shoot Winter Scenes: 24 Winter Photos to Feel the Romance of Snow

Published: 2022-12-15

Source link: https://www.cnphotos.net/academy/show-105.html

In winter, the thing I most want is to sit by the window in the afternoon, wrapped in a blanket, with a cup of coffee, and enjoy the afternoon sun. The 24 beautiful winter photos here are meant to make you feel the comfort of winter. Maybe you will even take your camera out into the snow to shoot amazing winter scenes!

1. A cozy cabin

Photo by Ian Keefe

This lakeside cabin in Canada looks quiet and calm, like a patch of warmth in a cold landscape. You know those social-media posts about spending a year in a cabin with no internet? This may be that cabin.

2. Hot coffee

Photo by Alex

On a cold snowy day, nothing is better than hot coffee, or hot chocolate or tea. Although this photo is desaturated and looks cold, it also gives a warm feeling.

3. Snow globe

Photo by Alex

Anyone would be glad to catch this moment! This frozen bubble, full of tiny snowflakes, rests on a branch, and the focus is perfect.

4. Winter hike

Photo by Donnie Rosie

This hemlock forest looks beautiful in the sunlight, under thick snow. Imagine walking through the woods.

5. A calm lake

Photo by Dominik Dombrowski

This quiet lake was shot in Germany. The water looks unusually calm, and the fog in the distance hides everything beyond the trees.

6. Misty forest

Photo by Christian Grab

This endless forest shows at a glance that winter has come. The snow-covered evergreens mark the start of winter, and the misty clouds give the photo mystery.

7. Neon and street

Photo by Josh Hild

No forest or lake here. This is a snowy night street. It was clearly shot long after the shops closed, and you can almost hear how quiet the city was.

8. A lonely cabin

Photo by Todd Diemer

Deep in the mountains of Norway is a ski town called Geilo, and here we see a cabin buried deep in snow. The photo makes the area look like a vast snowfield that never ends.

9. Cold balloons

Photo by yonatan anugerah

This snowy photo was shot in Cappadocia, Turkey. The air is full of hot-air balloons, which is not a traditional winter activity. So the elements look a little mismatched and novel.

10. A winter windowsill

Photo by Toa Heftiba

This is a warm snapshot of winter life. Drink a cup of your favorite hot tea, enjoy the comfort, and look out at the quiet snowy world.

11. A snow-covered forest

Photo by Jared Rice

Nothing beats an aerial drone shot. This forest photo gives a fresh view of a snowy forest. The snow looks incredibly smooth, as the trees rise from it like jagged rocks.

12. A city in heavy snow

Photo by Andre Benz

Although many people love the natural snow in the countryside, a city view in snow has its own taste.

13. A snow fox

Photo by Jonatan Pie

How could we leave a cute animal off this list? This lovely white fox is local to Iceland, and the photographer shot a beautiful photo here. The fox clearly likes to come for food each day, so it posed gracefully.

14. A flower on ice

Photo by Zoltan Tasi

Wow. Is this flower on the ice or under it? Either way, we see a stunning flower on the other side of bright ice, which makes this an impressive, absorbing photo.

15. Christmas

Photo by Roberto Nickson

This winter photo from Whistler, Canada, shows the Christmas mood. The street is full of festive elements, and snow-covered trees are lit up. It is cold, but happy.

16. Winter berries

Photo by Maria Shanina

The warm, bright colors contrast sharply with the white snow. This photo of bright red berries in the snow is simple but perfect.

17. Ice-field mountains

Photo by Alberto Restifo

Nothing is more beautiful than snow-covered peaks. This great ice field feels cold and barren, but also refreshing. Would it not be nice to walk here in snowshoes?

18. A forest cabin

Photo by Casey Horner

This is like a gingerbread cabin in a snowy forest. The fact that the subject is out of focus adds character to the photo. It feels blurry and relaxed.

19. Snow wrestle

Photo by Richard Burlton

No great forest, no calm lake, no cozy cabin. Just two dogs playing and wrestling in the snow. Animal photos never go wrong, and the action here is great.

20. Late night

Photo by Brayden Law

This street in Vancouver, Canada, may look a little rough, but you cannot help loving the orange and blue neon lights in the heavy snow. It is a great winter photo.

21. Arctic adventure

Photo by Markus Spiske

This photo has a good frame and good color. The green tones and the texture feel like a set from the 1980s sci-fi horror film The Thing.

22. Hooded up

Photo by Zhang Kenny

A mysterious person hides in the warm hood of this jacket. Against the winter background, the blank expression works well.

23. An empty road

Photo by Joris Molenaar

Speaking of loneliness, if the photo had one person instead of two, it would feel truly sad. The story of two people is different; it feels a little hopeful, though the colors feel a bit dim.

24. Skiing action

Photo by Johannes Andersson

Winter ski photos always look good. We see a skier stopped, with a cloud of snow swirling around. The sense of action in the center contrasts sharply with the silence of the snowy background.
