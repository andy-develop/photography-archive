Visual China Group and Zhipu Form a Strategic Partnership to Promote Innovative AI Use in Visual Content

Published: 2024-11-08

On November 8, Visual China Group announced that it joined the Series B6 equity financing of Beijing Zhipu Huazhang Technology Co., Ltd. (Zhipu). The two companies also formed a strategic partnership. They will work together to promote innovative uses of artificial intelligence in visual content. They will give users a smarter and more efficient visual experience.

Zhipu is a well-known Chinese large-model company that has moved quickly to commercialize its technology. It has built several model products that compare with OpenAI. It works with more than 2,000 companies and institutions. It has scaled up more than 1,000 large-model applications. It has also led or taken part in more than 100 government and industry research projects. Zhipu says that, using its large-model algorithms and technology, it will work with Visual China Group to build a visual large model based on copyright-compliant data. It will develop AI products for real industry uses, support creators together, and build a responsible and sustainable business model.

Visual China Group says this partnership with Zhipu on large-model data training and applications will make full use of leading AI, big data, and cloud computing. It will use the company's core strengths: a global creator community, large amounts of high-quality compliant content data, rich application scenarios, and experience in copyright operations in China. It will keep expanding business opportunities in AIGC, or AI-generated content. In the future, the two sides will explore more ways to work together. They will help the visual large model reach the market, and give users a wider range of AI products.

This partnership between Visual China Group and Zhipu shows that the two sides will work deeply together in AI technology and visual content. It also points to shared work to drive innovation and growth in the industry. It will bring new energy to the visual content sector.
