His Photos Never Show a Face, Yet They Have That High-End Feel All Over the Screen!

Published: 2022-03-13

Photographer Minh T was born in Vietnam and grew up on the U.S. East Coast. He works as both a photographer and a graphic design creative director, and he has a natural feel for geometric lines.

He is good at finding composition inside architectural spaces. Clean frames, soft and rich color, and portraits that are not about the face give his work a surreal romance.

"This minty moment" is a second account Minh T opened on Instagram, apart from his private account. The word minty comes from his name, Minh T. He started this "moment" because he wanted to create a fun place, a bright and pure fantasy world.

His work comes from the ordinary things in life that are easy to overlook. Through different points of view, he wants to show their real beauty.

"Most of my work is about finding the right place to shoot. So I like to drive around the edges of the city and look for buildings and landscapes that people have forgotten or overlooked.

The process is as fun as looking for a diamond in a pile of gravel."
