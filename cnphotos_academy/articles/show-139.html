City or Country, Desert or Rainforest: Beautiful Sunsets Are Everywhere!

Published: 2023-05-16

Original link: https://www.cnphotos.net/academy/show-139.html

Sunsets are a reliable, beautiful subject and backdrop for photos. Their beautiful change in light can make a boring scene suddenly feel magical.

But sunsets can also frustrate, especially for new photographers. When a sunset is especially beautiful, you may feel discouraged that you cannot capture all of its beauty in the camera.

We have gathered a few tips for shooting sunsets, along with some sunset photos taken at famous photography spots. Some are by professional photographers, and some by beginners, to show that you can take beautiful sunset photos at any level of experience.

City or country, desert or rainforest, beautiful sunsets are everywhere!

Bring a tripod

A tripod is a good choice whenever the light is low. Especially at the end of a sunset, your shutter needs to stay open longer to get a proper exposure. Without a tripod, you may take blurry photos in this light because of camera shake. Of course, you can also use a fixed object, such as a rock or a table, as a makeshift tripod.

Watch the clouds

If you want to catch a dramatic sunset, wait for clouds. A cloudless sunset sky can be beautiful, but it is not as dramatic as a cloudy sky. This is because the clouds scatter the sunlight and reveal its rich colors. Ideally, you want high or mid-level clouds, because these set off the colors of the sunset best.

Shoot reflections on water

Reflections are another way to add drama to a sunset photo.

By shooting on still water, you can widen the reach of the sunset and make it look more impressive.

Find a nice silhouette

For new photographers, a silhouette at sunset is a good starting point. It lets you practice composition without the frustration of an under-exposed foreground. Unlike an epic landscape under a sunset sky, you do not need filters or exposure blending to expose everything correctly. You can focus on composing the sunset and the silhouette, and learn the rest later.

Capture the sun's halo

You can get fascinating halo photos when the sun sinks below the clouds and the horizon. When you shoot a sun halo, try different aperture settings for different effects. A wide aperture like f/5.6 gives a soft halo, while a narrow aperture like f/22 gives a sharper one.

Shoot where the air is clean

Air pollution can create an interesting, hazy sunset with strong red tones, but it usually weakens the life of a sunset, because the pollution blocks the light. The most vivid sunsets usually happen where the lower atmosphere is clean, such as over the ocean. (This is one reason a beach is a great place for a sunset.) With less pollution, you will see bright colors instead of dull tones.

Dry weather

Like pollution, high humidity creates haze that blocks the bright colors of a sunset. This is why winter sunsets often look better than summer ones. Dry winter air makes the colors of a sunset look brighter. But if you do not like shooting sunsets in the snow, you can also catch a beautiful sunset in autumn, when the weather is cooler and drier than in summer, and the cold has not yet set in.

Compose carefully

Shooting a gorgeous subject like a sunset does not automatically make your photo look gorgeous. Even with the right gear and camera settings, a poor composition will only give you a boring image. Good composition is the key to a beautiful photo.

Be more creative

Sunsets are beautiful, but they have become a cliche subject in photography. There are so many sunset photos. How can you make yours stand out? This is where creativity comes in. With technical skill and a creative mind, you can rise above the cliche and create images no one has seen before.
