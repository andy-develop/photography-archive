A Bit Bold: The Body Art Photographed by a Foreign Woman Photographer Is Very Daring

Published: 2022-05-20

How bold the person,

that big is the harvest of creativity.

Can you imagine that in daily life

vegetables are made into bags?

What do they look like?

Believe it or not,

they look quite novel.

These three fresh bags suit summer well.

They are made from real asparagus, cucumber, and cabbage.

It seems you can almost smell, through the screen,

the smell of the vegetables.

The designer of these vegetable bags

was probably inspired while strolling a market

or eating a salad.

It seems food not only stimulates the taste buds,

it also sparks a designer's inspiration.

Gab Bois

A Canadian photography artist

from Montreal, Canada.

She has long used everyday objects to reshape fashion.

She has attracted nearly 560,000 followers on Instagram.

For example, a top made of red cabbage.

Believe it or not,

The unique color and texture of the vegetable

add a fashion feel to the top.

To turn your hobby into a career

and still keep the love and passion,

I think

must be a very happy thing.

Her colors are full of energy,

and highly saturated.

She often searches her home

for settings and objects she can create with.

From the kitchen to the living room, bedroom, and bathroom,

she keeps observing and thinking,

and comes up with bold creative ideas.

Even though many online readers question her,

and think she is making a joke,

or that this is not art at all,

she only grows bolder with each setback.

She hopes her work

will make people think.

These playful ideas of hers

come half from her father, a painter.

Her father paints surrealism,

and this gives her strong support in her work.

Even though there are many harsh comments and doubts,

she says:

I just keep doing what I want to do.

Time will prove the rest.
