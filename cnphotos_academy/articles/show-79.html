Stuck With Bad Photos? Bresson Gives You 21 Pieces of Advice

Published: 2022-08-18

Original link: https://www.cnphotos.net/academy/show-79.html

       When people speak of Bresson, no photographer is unfamiliar with him. In his life, Bresson took a large body of weighty documentary and news work. He also opposed cropping photos and using flash, because he held that a photographer should not interfere with the light of the scene. Bresson is seen as one of the greatest photographers of the 20th century, the founder of modern photojournalism. His street candids influenced a whole generation of young photographers. He is called the "father of modern photojournalism" and was one of the founders of Magnum Photos.

1. "It is an illusion that photos are made with the camera. They are made with the eye, the heart, and the head."

2. "Your first 10,000 photographs are your worst."

3. "A photograph is not obtained by force. It happens of itself. It is the photograph that takes you, not you that take the photograph."

4. "Of all the means of expression, photography is the only one that fixes a precise moment in time."

5. "The quality of a photo is decided the moment it is taken."

6. "To me, photography is the simultaneous recognition, in a fraction of a second, of the significance of an event."

7. "To me, photography is an instant impulse, born of an eye that catches the moment and the eternal."

8. "Your eye must see the composition and expression that life itself offers, and by instinct you must know when to press the shutter."

9. "To me, the camera is a sketchbook, an instrument of intuition and spontaneity."

10. "Memory is very important. The memory of a photo unfolds at the same speed as the event. While you work, you must leave no gaps and record everything, because after that it is all too late."

11. "The photographer deals with things that vanish ceaselessly, and when they vanish, no device on earth can bring them back."

12. "In life, the awareness of appearance, outline, and value is, for me, the essence of photography. Composition should be a constant aim, a simultaneous integration, an organic harmony of visual elements."

13. "But we must avoid taking light, thoughtless shots that pile up needless images on ourselves. These images disturb our memory and lower the overall clarity."

14. "The photographer must always, to the greatest degree, respect his own point of view."

15. "In photography, the smallest thing can be a great subject. The human detail can become the main theme."

16. "As time passes, people come back to you as you look at a portrait, like a silent echo. Photography is the trace left by a face in transformation. Photography is linked to death. It is a trace."

17. "For me, photography is a means of understanding, not separate from other visual means of expression. It is a way to shout and release yourself, not to prove or claim your own originality. It is a way of life."

18. "You should think before and after the shot, not during it."

19. "Photography is an instant reaction. Painting is a meditation."

20. "The heavy use of photos by the mass media brings new duties to the photographer. We must admit the gap between the economic needs of consumer society and the needs of people who witness this age. This affects all of us, especially young photographers. We must be more careful than ever not to cut ourselves off from the real world and from other people."

21. "You just have to live, and life will give you photos."
