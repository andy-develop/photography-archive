Capturing the Beauty of Tacheng: Winners of the 2026 "Oil Painting Tacheng, Silk Road Charm" Chinese and Foreign Photography Contest Announced

Published: 2026-06-18

In 2026, Visual China, together with the Publicity Department of the Tacheng Prefectural Party Committee in Xinjiang, officially launched the 2026 "Oil Painting Tacheng, Silk Road Charm" Chinese and Foreign Photography Contest. This contest centers on Tacheng's unique natural scenery, human culture, and development. It invites Chinese and foreign photographers to record the colorful charm of this Silk Road hub city with their cameras.

The contest opened on January 8 and closed on May 18. It received more than 20,000 entries. After rounds of selection, 50 winning sets stood out. The list of winners is officially announced today. Let us enjoy these striking images that capture the beauty of Tacheng.
