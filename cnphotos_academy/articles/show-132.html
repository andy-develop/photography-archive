Learning to Use the Foreground Is the Real Secret to Great Landscape Photos

Published: 2023-04-15

Original link: https://www.cnphotos.net/academy/show-132.html

Composition is the foundation of photography. Every strong photo is built on composition.Take landscape photography. The foreground matters a great deal.It can make or break a photo. How do you use it?Read the full summary below.

First, enjoy a set of works by the photographer Patrick. These grand landscapes show the pull of foreground-led composition.

This foreground-led way of guiding the eye greatly strengthens the impact of the frame. It layers the near view into the far view, full and rich. It feels like a steady undercurrent that stirs the heartbeat, grand yet refined.

So how do you use this composition well? The detailed notes are below:


  I. Develop an eye for lines  

1. Find regular lines

Lines are the most direct way to strengthen the impact of a composition. They suit both the tension of the lens and the mood of the scene.When you frame the shot, watch closely. Shoot from different angles, and dig out regular lines.

2. Use a single line well

A single line gives the frame a forward visual pull. When you use this technique, get close to the subject with a wide angle from a low point.

  II. Build the area from points  

1. Find sparse, scattered points

Choose sparse, scattered points to lead the composition. Points join into lines, and points with lines form an area. It makes the frame feel tightly linked.

  III. Composition methods  

1. Triangle composition

When the foreground is scattered and takes up much of the frame, triangle composition gives the frame a stable center. It does not look messy.

2. Frame composition

Frame composition is highly recognizable. If you handle the texture poorly, the whole frame is ruined. Use it with care if your post-processing is weak.
