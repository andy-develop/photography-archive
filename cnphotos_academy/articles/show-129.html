Do you wonder how those photo pros actually take their shots?

The Spanish photographer Jordi Koalitic posted a series of behind-the-scenes clips on Ins. Viewers said it turns out you only need a cheap effect to shoot a blockbuster.

Jordi Koalitic is a well-known influencer photographer on Ins. Look at his followers and you know his skill.

Jordi Koalitic shares his behind-the-scenes work so more people can learn photography and shoot more creative photos.

Sometimes the most ordinary everyday props, once Jordi Koalitic reworks them, become the decisive prop of a blockbuster.

For example, the water bottle we drink from every day, once you cut off the bottom, lets you try many tricks.

Most people only use a phone to take photos. But did you know a phone can be a prop? Take two phones and fold them in front of the lens. The effect is simply amazing. Follow the China Photo Network official account: wwwcnphotosnet for more tips and work news.

In a season without snow, a piece of broken foam can create a winter effect of falling snow.

Fasten fresh flowers around the outer ring of the lens, and you can shoot a dreamy flower portrait.

The mop you use to clean the floor, from a different camera angle, can also give a fresh, clean photo.

And the once-popular fruit-slicing photos are actually made by posing.

The guitar and the notes combine perfectly. It is truly full of literary style.

The soft ring at home, stretched out, can also give all kinds of creative photos.

Clear glass is also a great prop. Keep changing the angle, and you always get a new creative photo.

With the same idea, you can also have the model lie under the glass, and get a different portrait.

The fridge shot and the washing-machine shot are actually selfies taken by putting the camera inside. Is it not simple?

Also, shooting with a long exposure will never disappoint you.

You can also draw circles and shake next to the lens. The light trails that appear are just as beautiful.
