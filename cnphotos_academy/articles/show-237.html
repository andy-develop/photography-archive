IntroductionToday the travel market is growing fast. Clients expect more speed and a better experience from travel photography. The Xuanpian online photo-selection platform, soon to be launched by Hunan Xiangxiang Technology Co., Ltd., solves the slow, poor delivery of traditional travel photography. It brings a new smart experience to photographers and clients.

The slow workflow of traditional travel-photo deliveryThe traditional delivery workflow is clumsy and slow. Clients wait in line for their shoot. The photographer or assistant takes payment and records contact details before the shoot. During the shoot, they note the starting file number so they can tell files apart later. After the shoot, the photographer imports photos and video to a computer for editing, and sorts them by number. Once editing is done, the work goes to the client by WeChat, QQ, or a cloud drive. This workflow takes time and is easy to get wrong, so the client experience is poor.

The smart solution that Xuanpian bringsThe Xuanpian online photo-selection platform uses new technology to fully change the old delivery model. While the photographer shoots, Xuanpian reads the camera's content in real time and uploads it to the cloud. With AI retouching, photos and video are processed on their own. Clients can browse the shoot live on their own phones.

The platform uses screenshot-blocking and dynamic watermark technology to protect the copyright of the work. Clients can only browse content before they pay. They cannot take screenshots or download. Xuanpian also offers online selection and paid download. Photographers can set package prices for clients to choose. Once a client pays, the download access unlocks.

Faster work, a better experienceXuanpian not only makes photographers work faster. It also greatly improves the client experience. Clients do not wait a long time. They see the shoot live, and use online selection and paid download to easily get the photos they want. For photographers, Xuanpian cuts the clumsy manual work, lowers the error rate, and gives stronger copyright protection.
