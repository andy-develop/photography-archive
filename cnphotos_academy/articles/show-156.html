From Commercial Success to a Major Breakthrough in Smart Photography: The Path of Song Tao's Photography Practice

Published: 2023-11-01

Original link: https://www.cnphotos.net/academy/show-156.html

'Technology is changing every industry, and it is also deeply affecting China's photography industry.' Song Tao said this in an earlier interview with the media. Because of this clear understanding, Song Tao decided to keep studying on the path of exploring smart photography technology.

In five years, Song Tao opened 30 photography stores. At the age of 26, the brand he founded received investment from CDH Investments, a major private equity firm, at a valuation of 200 million yuan. At the age of 30, Song Tao built a family fun and growth center called Baobao Dangjia. At 33, he explored again and founded Meishi Meike, an online-to-offline photography service platform. Song Tao's commercial success in the photography industry is well known. His constant innovation in shooting techniques and business models has kept leading the industry forward. At the same time, his influence in smart photography technology has grown wider as his company, Shitang Technology, has developed.

When internet technology first began to grow fast in China, Song Tao sensed the trend early. In 2015 he developed the Meishi Meike app. He used innovative internet thinking to reshape the ecosystem of the photography industry. He won large capital support, and he also attracted many companies to build similar photography software.

Commercial success made Song Tao see more clearly the power of advanced technology in the photography industry. It also made him more determined to keep studying photography technology. For this reason, Song Tao became one of the first researchers in China to join artificial intelligence (AI) with photography. In 2020, in an interview with East Money, he said his goal was to use AI to give users an entertaining experience and professional photo services. To reach this goal, Song Tao has kept working on the most advanced smart technology and new photography methods. In 2019 he founded Beijing Shitang Technology Company. He has already built parts of a smart photography system with very successful test results. It has had some influence in the industry, and he is called the 'leader of smart photography in China.' His patent and technology for a smart, unmanned selfie booth drew wide attention in the industry. In the same year, Canon approached Song Tao. After talks, the two sides formed a deep partnership to build the 'Paila Paila Smart Professional Selfie Booth.' This solution became a classic and successful industry application for Canon in information technology. It is widely known in the field.

Long research and practice made Song Tao a senior expert in applying smart photography technology. He has studied image processing and other photography-related techniques in depth. Besides the 'AI smart photography system,' he also built 'AI automatic photo editing and image optimization software.' He also built the more advanced 'big-data shooting analysis and automatic parameter setting system.' These programs combine deep AI technology. They are highly open and compatible. This smart photography software can run as a stand-alone back-end system. It also allows easy deep secondary development, so developers can upgrade and maintain it with very clear logic. These technologies give Song Tao great influence in the field. They also keep 'Paila Paila' ahead of other products in the industry.

As Song Tao hopes, 'Paila Paila' is not only a smart selfie booth. He wants the 'Paila Paila' smart selfie booth to be every user's 'smart photography company.' Besides indoor photography, he also wants to achieve smart shooting in outdoor real scenes. He is still studying this in depth. Song Tao says the technologies already used in 'Paila Paila' are proven mature and reliable. Besides selfie booths, these technologies have been tested in outdoor security, unmanned photography, smart industrial robots, and other fields. The 'Paila Paila' technology has broad potential for industry applications.

Song Tao says the AI wave now sweeps the whole world, and smart photography technology is part of it. As a researcher and innovator in smart photography, he is full of confidence in the future. Smart photography technology will keep shaping the development of the industry. He will keep studying and exploring the front edge of smart photography. He will keep innovating and apply it to more fields.
