A Bird's-Eye View: They Captured Ground Beauty You Have Never Seen

Published: 2022-01-10

The same thing, seen from a different angle, always yields something new.

When brothers JP Andrew and Mike Andrew first shot with a drone, they were stunned by the view from the air.

The first abstract aerial art photo the Andrew brothers shot

The first drone the Andrew brothers used

Seen from the air, the landscapes that look ordinary on the ground become magical and beautiful.

Inspired by this, the brothers began to travel the world and decided to use their work to tell viewers how incredible this world looks from the air. This is how this series of abstract aerial art was born.

The Andrew brothers' aerial images are marked by clever composition. They rely on no luck at all.

Before they arrive to shoot on site, they usually use Google Earth and other map software to build a virtual image on a computer first.

In the images below, the left picture is a Google Earth capture and the right picture is the actual shot.

Beyond scouting on site and building images in Google Earth, choosing the right time and light is also very important for the Andrew brothers.

For example, the image called Skyline shows a cargo ship sailing on the water.

The shadow cast by the containers on the ship onto the water looks like a city skyline, and it catches the eye at once.

Skyline

Though some of the aerial images we shoot look hard to believe, and even raise questions about their authenticity, each one really is a real place on our planet.

The Andrew brothers say that, apart from slight color and contrast enhancement, their photos have no other artificial processing.

They hope these photos tell viewers that a whole new world on this planet is waiting to be discovered.

Whether it is cars parked in symmetry or colorful parasols by the sea, the interesting arrangements they find are a pleasure for the viewer.

The more beauty we discover, the more we feel we have only scratched the surface.
