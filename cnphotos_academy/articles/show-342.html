Pixel Cake Enables Tethered Shooting with the Hasselblad X2D II 100C, Speeding Up the Medium Format Workflow

Published: 2026-07-16

2026 7 16, Pixel Cake officially announced support for HasselbladX2D II 100Ccameras for wired tethered shooting. This tethering support lets this flagship device, with its1megapixel ultra-high resolution,16-bit color depth,15.3and class-leading wide dynamic range, for the first time sync on-camera shooting and instant transfer on both computer and mobile, with on-site output. It breaks the efficiency bottleneck of the traditional high-end medium format workflow and fully upgrades the professional medium format creation process.

 

As a legendary Swedish camera brand, Hasselblad has long served top-tier photography scenes with its ultimate image quality. HasselbladX2D II 100Cis the benchmark device for high-end commercial creation. In the past it relied on traditional standalone shooting, and the tethering workflow was relatively fixed. This time Pixel Cake and Hasselblad linked their technology to expand tethered-shooting options for flagship models and greatly optimize the high-end shooting pipeline.

This adaptation upgrades full-device teamwork. It fully supports wired tethering onMac、Windows、iPad、iPhoneand Android devices, and fully supports HasselbladRAWformat tethered shooting. The computer andiPadside can read camera parameters in real time, trigger the shutter remotely, quickly transfer shot files back, and support RAW image preview. With Pixel Cake's mature technology, photographers do not need to convert files by hand or switch between software, forming an integrated loop of“shooting-tethering-retouching”an integrated loop, greatly improving on-site creation and delivery efficiency for high-end commercial shooting.

 

This successful adaptation of a flagship model is the result of Pixel Cake's long focus on the tethered-shooting track. The brand relies on strengths such as multi-model compatibility, multi-scene tethering modes, and its self-developed16 bit ·AI Rawreal-time color grading engine, to build a stable and professional imaging technology base, which let it complete this cooperation with Hasselblad. The launch of this feature is also an important step for Chinese imaging technology toward internationalization and professionalism, and it shows the top R&D strength of local imaging software.

This tethering adaptation is the first result of the two sides' cooperation. Going forward, Pixel Cake and Hasselblad will keep deepening their cooperation, keep iterating and upgrading the feature experience, offer more advanced linked modes, and keep improving the high-end medium format imaging creation ecosystem.
