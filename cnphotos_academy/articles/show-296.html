Winners Announced | GCPA Da Jiang Cup · 5th Global Huashe International Photography Competition

Published: 2025-12-24

Co-hosted by the Global Chinese Photographers Federation (GCPA) and the Dali International Photography Exhibition (DIPE),GCPA Da Jiang Cup · 5th Global Huashe International Photography Competition,, after carrying the authoritative certification of the Global Photographic Union (GPU) and the Canadian Association for Photographic Art (CAPA), followed months of worldwide submissions and several rounds of rigorous online review by an international jury, and has now named its winning works.

This edition received nearly 11,000 entries from photographers in 25 countries and regions. In the end, 457 winning works were chosen across seven categories: Color Open, Black and White Open, Nature, Color Landscape (with drone), Human Theme and Portrait, Urban Life and Street, and AI New Image Creation. These works show the breadth and depth of photography through diverse viewpoints, sincere feeling, and outstanding image language. All winning works will be posted and shared here in installments. Please stay tuned.

We sincerely thank photography clubs around the world, media organizations at home and abroad, and all official-account platforms for their strong support and promotion of this competition.

We thank photography lovers everywhere for taking part and for their warm submissions.

We salute the international and domestic jurors for their professional, rigorous, and fair review. We send our sincere thanks and respect to all the volunteers who quietly gave their time and energy to the competition.

Special thanks to Da Jiang Wine, Pintang Culture and Art, and Hahnemuhle Germany for their title sponsorship and for sponsoring the offline exhibition of the winning works.

We look forward to seeing you at the Dali International Photography Exhibition in June 2026!

We will post the winning works of each category in turn. Please keep following, and enjoy the images of the world.
