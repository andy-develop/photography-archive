At the Exhibition: Lishui World Photography Conference: Looking at the World, the Local, and Photography

Published: 2022-11-21

Original link: https://www.cnphotos.net/academy/show-104.html

On November 17, 2022, the 2022 World Photography Conference opened in Lishui, Zhejiang. The theme this year is 'World Images, Lishui Coordinates.' Several photo exhibitions, focused on 'world cities,' 'the local in Zhejiang,' 'the art of photography,' and 'the power of youth,' are on view for local residents and for photography enthusiasts from across the country. They let people see the world and see what is around them through photography.

The exhibition 'City of the World,' at the Lishui Art Museum, centers on the theme of cities. Through an open call to the world, it finally shows 548 photos by 252 photographers and institutions, covering 215 cities in 73 countries and regions. The exhibition is divided into nine sections. Through a rich range of approaches, such as documentary, creative, conceptual, and pictorial work, it shows the look of the most representative cities of East and West. It reflects urban space, human history, social life, and the natural ecology of countries around the world. 'Through this exhibition, we can see the look of places around the world without leaving home. At the same time, the exhibition lets more places be seen by everyone. We hope this project can continue at every World Photography Conference, and become an important window for countries around the world to show the face of their cities,' said Wang Xueke, executive curator of the curatorial team for the 'City of the World' global group exhibition, to reporters.

The Zhejiang Photographic Art Exhibition, at the oil pump factory venue, is part of the Zhejiang Photography Art Week that opened at the same time. It shows the different viewpoints of photographers from Zhejiang. For example, the photographer Wu De uses a series of photos to show the history of change at the Shangyu Cotton Mill, a large state-owned enterprise with 53 years of history. Zhou Xiangyang, a man from Jiashan, kept photographing scenes that interested him and gathered them into a story of a Jiangnan water town. Shu Qiaomin used portrait and documentary work to show the Lishui rural spring festival, where a folk celebration brings everyday life onto the stage. These works, which look at the ways of life, historical memory, and local customs of their own regions, are both familiar to their makers. They also let viewers see, through images, the past and present of Zhejiang's towns and villages, and the changes in society and life within them. 'The provincial exhibition shows the strength of the Zhejiang team of photographers. It encourages Zhejiang photographers to root themselves in the local, go deeper into the local, and rise above the local, and to tell the Zhejiang story of the two pioneering moves in a systematic way through images,' said Mao Xiaofang, resident vice chairman of the Zhejiang Photographers Association.

The Lishui Photography Museum shows the shortlisted works of the ninth 'China Photography Annual Ranking.' The 'China Photography Annual Ranking' is a branded photography event that Lishui launched for the whole country. This year, ten domestic artists made the list. Among them are mature artists who have already built their own photographic language (such as Shi Guowei, whose photo-painting works are hand-colored; Yang Yongliang, who built a 'digital landscape' by combining Eastern and Western aesthetics; and Huang Qingjun, who shot 'A Streamer's Belongings' using staged sets that combine portraits and collected objects). There are also new forces that have emerged in recent years (such as Chen Ronghui, whose staged works rethink the truth of photography and historical archives and its results; and Guo Jiaxi, who photographed the rural landscape of Lingshan and used handmade books to spatialize photography). These works show, from one side, the professional level and the trend of photography in China, and they play a positive academic leading role in the exploration of Chinese photography. Fu Weixin, director of the Lishui Photography Museum, said: 'From emotional expression to the clash of ideas, from technical breakthrough to the presentation of exhibitions, this year's ranking reflects the very front edge of image art in China.'

The 2022 Zhejiang Youth Photography Exhibition, at the Lishui Art Museum, was held for the first time. It was split off from the youth section of the former Zhejiang Photographic Art Exhibition. It uses a new operating model, different from the provincial exhibition. The whole process, from creating, output, and production to mounting the show, was done independently by the 36 selected young photographers, and the final judging took place on site. It is an experimental innovation. The exhibition focuses on the young photographic talent of Zhejiang, and sketches the young face of Zhejiang photography. 'The forms of these works are very diverse and rich,' said Jiang Wei, a photography critic and one of the judges, summing up. 'For more than ten years, the Zhejiang Photographers Association has placed great importance on nurturing new photographic talent. They dare to explore photography and have a style all their own, and this exhibition proves it.'

It is reported that the 2022 World Photography Conference is co-hosted by the Lishui Municipal People's Government and the International Federation of Photographic Art. From this year on, Lishui will be the permanent home of the conference, which is held every two years. With the aim of 'professionalism, industrial scale, and international reach,' it aims to build a professional platform that shows the cutting edge of photographic technology, a display platform that brings together the works of international masters, a business platform that gathers the high-end elements of the image industry, and a shared platform that draws Chinese and young visitors to Lishui. The related exhibitions run until dates between December 15 and 31.
