Photography experience:

He worked at the Changhua branch of the Lin'an County Cultural Center, and then at the Lin'an District Federation of Literary and Art Circles in Hangzhou. For many years he photographed subjects such as people with disabilities. He has held solo themed photography exhibitions many times, in China and abroad.

Photographic works:

Series of 20 portraits, Our Portraits, 1985-2022

China now has more than 85 million people with disabilities. They make up about 6.21% of the total population. Behind these cold numbers are families that carry heavy burdens. They are also people who hope for a better life. Above all, they are people whose bodies are disabled but whose will is strong, and who stand on their own. This set of environmental portraits records 20 outstanding people with disabilities from many fields in China (including a self-portrait of the author). The work spans 37 years. We face the hard turns that physical disability brings. We do not blame heaven or other people. We fight our fate, hold dreams in our hearts, and embrace the world. Disabled in body, strong in will is the word we live by. On each journey to chase a dream, we keep our feet on the ground and move forward. Yet each of us has truly felt the hardship behind the struggle. We have also felt the joy of seizing the throat of fate. In the process of this long project, I captured plain, true images. They show a positive, upward face. Through these images, I want to hold up a large mirror. I want the viewer, by comparison, to form a new self-knowledge and gain something from it.

Themed series of 30 images, Under the Same Blue Sky, 2006-2020

In our lives, there is a group of people who live in this world with disabled bodies. They bear pain that ordinary people cannot know. They go through countless hardships. But they do not bow to fate. Under a strong socialist system, under the same blue sky, in the embrace of their motherland, they use an exceptional will to overcome many difficulties. They keep their feet on the ground, set their dreams free, and still live their own meaningful lives. I am a photographer without hands, but I love photography deeply. I feel, as if it were my own, the way people with disabilities push past the limits of their bodies and their lives. It is my responsibility to show their stories, of disabled bodies and strong will, and of stubborn struggle, through images and to spread them. For decades I have kept focus on this subject. At my own cost, as an ordinary person, I visited more than 100 counties and towns in China. I completed portraits of more than 100 outstanding people with disabilities. I held solo shows, published photo books, and placed work in the media. These show the self-reliant spirit of Chinese people with disabilities. This series selects moments in the work, study, and daily life of 30 people with disabilities. It spans 15 years. Each subject is an outstanding representative among Chinese people with disabilities. In my work, I freeze the instant and also care about the look of the image. I seek natural beauty and pass on positive energy. Through these images, I want to tell everyone: every person in this world, as long as they do not fear hardship or the storm, you and I under the same blue sky, will together build a meaningful life.
