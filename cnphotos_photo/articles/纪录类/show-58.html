Selected Works of the 19th Zhejiang Provincial Photographic Art Exhibition, Documentary Category (including Long-term Observation Unit)

Published: 2022-10-10 Category: Documentary Original: https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-58.html

The Zhejiang Provincial Photographic Art Exhibition is held by the Zhejiang Photographers Association,and it is the oldest, most influential, and largestprovincial photography exhibition. It has run 18 sessions. It has produced many fine works and discovered many photographers. It fully shows the achievements and overall face of Zhejiang photography today.

The 19th Zhejiang Provincial Photographic Art Exhibition(hereafter the Provincial Exhibition) is guided by Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era. It upholds the Two Directions, the Hundred Flowers policy, and the Two Innovations principles. It calls on photographers to keep pace with the times, stay centered on the people, offer fine works to the people, and use sound values to guide social trends. It contributes photographic strength to the Eight-Eight Strategy, to building an important window, to striving for a leading province of socialist modernization, and to building a demonstration zone for common prosperity through high-quality development.

This session is hosted by the Zhejiang Federation of Literary and Art Circles, the Zhejiang Photographers Association, and the Propaganda Department of the Lishui Municipal Party Committee.The Lishui Federation of Literary and Art Circles, the Lishui Photographers Association, and the Lishui PhotographyMuseum organize it.The Liandu District Federation ofLiterary and Art Circles co-organize it. The show opened on May 1, 2022. Submissions closed on August 8. Closed judging ran September 26 to 27.Public review ran online October 11 to 18. Final results were announced October 24. It opens in Lishui on November 18.

We look forward to meeting you there!
