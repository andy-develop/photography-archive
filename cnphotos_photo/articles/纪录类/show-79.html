Photography works:

Series of 20 frames, “Fellow Villagers of the Central Plains”

The Central Plains once meant “the great plain at the center of all under heaven,” and later it mainly meant the middle and lower reaches of the Yellow River. It is the birthplace of Chinese civilization. As history moves on, Central Plains culture has always shown a strong power to renew itself, to embrace, to absorb, and to endure. Since the reform and opening up, the ways people work and live here have changed beyond recognition. Traditional culture meets and clashes with modern popular culture, and it takes on a rich, varied set of forms. I grew up in the Central Plains, and I have a special bond with this land and the people who live here. I was lucky to live through a time of leapfrog change. As a photographer, for thirty-five years I have recorded and felt it with care. Through the daily life of Central Plains people, I keep a sense of home and bear witness to development.

Feature series of 30 frames, “City Expressions at Special Moments”

A city is a dense concentration of human society at a given stage. The ways people work and live in it show the city's spirit and its level of civilization. Hosting a major event shows the full strength of a city and gives strong momentum to its growth. It has a unique effect on soft power and on raising the city's name and influence. It makes the city more confident and the people happier. This series focuses on representative Chinese cities and major historical events. It records landmark city scenes and the people in those events. From a small detail it reads the big picture, and from one point it draws out a whole. It links city memories into a national memory of growth and progress. 2008 Beijing Olympics. 2010 Shanghai World Expo. 2010 Guangzhou Asian Games. 2014 Nanjing Youth Olympic Games. 2021 Xi'an National Games. 2022 Beijing Winter Olympics.
