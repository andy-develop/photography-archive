Photographs:

Series of 10: Line 4

Line 4 is one of the busiest lines in the Beijing subway, and it is the line I take to work every day. It links the dense districts of Haidian, Xicheng, Fengtai, and Daxing. It runs past famous shopping streets, historic sites, and science parks. Because it passes Tsinghua, Peking, and Renmin universities, people call it the scholar line. The subway gives strangers a shared journey. Different lives pass through this closed space. There is warmth and coldness, joy and sorrow. I always feel the pulse of the city and the drive of people striving. Later I photographed other lines, but Line 4 stayed a symbol to me. As of June 2022, Beijing rail network reached 783.1 kilometers, with over ten million rides a day, the most in the world. The lines keep growing, and the growth gives people confidence and hope for a better life.

Series of 20: Our Daughter

Like most parents, I used photos and videos to record my daughter growing up, and I kept at it for 26 years. My daughter Qiaoqiao is an ordinary city girl, and I photographed only everyday moments. But the troubles she faced while growing up, such as arts education, homework pressure, teenage rebellion, school exams, and the craze for Korean culture, are common to this generation against the backdrop of China development. For that reason, this record reaches beyond one family and takes on social meaning. Parents love their children selflessly and want them to grow up as they imagine. But each life is its own and cannot be designed. Children usually make their own choices. Having more choices is itself a sign of social progress.

Themed series of 20: Sharing One Global Village

Photography took me farther. In every corner of the world, I found both sunlight and shadow. I saw different landscapes and tasted the full range of human life. Happy families are all alike. Every unhappy family is unhappy in its own way. But whatever the life, it holds the whole of living: hard times, and the right to seek happiness and to enjoy joy. The world is not an island. We share one global village and one fate. May the land be free of famine and war. May hearts be free of fear. May the world be full of laughter. Not every hope comes true. But through struggle and reflection, people help one another and move on.
