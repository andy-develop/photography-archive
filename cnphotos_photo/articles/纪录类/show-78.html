Photography career:

Deputy secretary-general of the Guangdong Photographers Association. He has worked in photography at the Xinjiang Economic Daily, the Guangdong New Express, and other outlets.

Photographic works:

10-photo series "The People Will Not Forget"

10-photo series "The Tech Elite of China's Heavy Equipment"

The makers of China's heavy equipment

10-photo series "The First Secretaries for Poverty Relief in Guangdong"

His 2021 portrait series "The First Secretaries for Poverty Relief in Guangdong" toured several cities in Guangdong as part of the photo exhibition "Good Harvest, Happy People: A Song Along the Long Plateau." The exhibition marked poverty relief and the building of a moderately prosperous society.

20-photo feature "The Republic Will Not Forget"

In 2011, on the 90th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China, he directed and shot a large photo feature by the New Express photo department called "Image Archive of 100 Veteran Party Members." The series took portraits of 100 veteran party members and cadres who made outstanding contributions to the republic or earned distinguished military honors. The reports ran in the New Express, and a large photo exhibition opened on July 1, the 90th anniversary.
