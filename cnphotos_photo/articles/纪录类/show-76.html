Photographs:

Photo essay of 30 images: Canopy Flowers Remember the Past, Notes on Forty Years of Photographing Paratroopers

2022 marked the 70th anniversary of the death of the martyr Huang Jiguang. By then I had spent 40 years photographing paratroopers as they jumped and trained on the ground. Someone once asked me why I felt such a fondness for photographing paratroopers. The answer is that this is a heroic force that cannot be broken or worn down, and it has a glorious tradition. These are people I truly respect. During the 5.12 Wenchuan earthquake, 15 paratroopers made a life-risking jump. They crossed a forbidden zone of life and drew a line of hope across a death zone where a jump was not safe. I watched the soldiers work day and night. They flew more than 1,000 kilometers in an emergency airlift. More than 2,000 soldiers carried out a rescue between life and death. With their own hands and backs, they brought hope to the people in the disaster area. With bloodied shoulders, they carried the duty that the Party and the people gave them. The paratroopers survived the shaking earth and wrote a heroic story. The paratroopers live with their parachutes like migratory birds. They travel across the vast land of the country all year round. Through spring, summer, autumn, and winter, through wind, frost, rain, and snow, across snowy plateaus, broad deserts, open grasslands, and mountain ridges, they leave their footprints. Once they receive an order, no matter how bad the weather or how hard the conditions, they leap into the roar of the warplanes, embrace the sky, and pursue their mission. Canopies open across the sky and land in the assigned airspace. The canopies bring back the past. This force is a heroic one. They always train themselves in hard conditions. They always reach new heights as they build a stronger army. They always serve as the vanguard in emergency operations. Most of all, they stay ready like an arrow on the string. They take on heavy duties when the country needs them. They step forward when the people are in danger. This force has the skill to deploy, land, and win. It is an airborne iron fist that always prevails. It proves that at the key moment when the nation needs it, it can strike like a drawn blade and hit the vital point of the enemy. In the new era, with their canopies beside them, they will carry forward the red legacy and write new glory.

Series of 10 images: My Comrades, My Soldiers, Notes on Paratrooper Hunter Training

Within the paratrooper camp, new recruits arrive and old soldiers leave, year after year. The barracks stay fixed, but the soldiers flow through. When they arrive as new recruits, everyone smiles. When they leave as veterans, everyone cries. When new recruits first arrive, the army feels hard. When veterans leave, the memories are all sweet. At first, comrade is just a name. The young soldier says a shy hello to his comrade. Slowly, comrade becomes a feeling. He calls out comrade and means brother. In time, as the years pass, comrade becomes an everlasting memory. It stands for those years and the group of people who fought together. I remember them standing guard in the wind, keeping watch in the rain, crawling through mud, fighting in rivers, crossing on ropes, climbing steep cliffs, firing on the move, scouting in the dark, and finding food to survive in the wild. Wherever they are, they use their effort, sweat, tears, blood, and sacrifice to build a solid wall in the air and on the ground. In the peace that has no gunsmoke, at the moment of national crisis, my dear comrades have never been absent. Whenever and wherever, in every season, they move like migratory birds. They fight from north to south, or go deep into the desert, to the northern border, to forest and snowfields. They join flood relief, earthquake relief, major drills, and important ceremonies. They seem to chase the spray of the long river of history, without end. Countless moving stories flow in their chests. In these 365 days, they have few days of rest. For the sake of the country's iron wall, and for the long peace and happiness of the people, they do not hesitate to bleed, sweat, or even die. Under a blue sky and on solid ground, they train for battle at every moment and live for the people. They always show the bearing and spirit of the strong. If war comes, they will win with one move, strike with one sword, and charge forward. The comrades of the new era continue the glory of their age in another way.

Series of 10 images: An Amdo Tibetan Wedding

China is a unified multi-ethnic country with a long history. The Tibetan people are an important part of the Chinese nation and Chinese culture. In the past, Tibet moved in a single leap from a feudal serf society based on highland herding to a socialist society where all people are equal. Now, as a moderately prosperous society is fully built, Tibetan people have left poverty behind and gained a full and comfortable material life. For thousands of years, Tibetan people have passed on their culture across the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau. They created a rich, bright, and lasting culture of their own. The Tibetan wedding customs that go back to ancient times still shine through centuries of change and the modern world. In 2017, I traveled with two comrades to Maqu County in the Gannan Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture. By chance, we came upon a Tibetan wedding. The groom, 30, was named Tashi Rabtan. The bride, 22, was named Lhamo Kyi. They met as teacher and student, and their bond grew into a lifelong promise. They were warm and hospitable. They invited us to the wedding. They drank with us at the same table, and they pulled us into the lively Guozhuang circle dance. During the wedding, I saw old farming-life customs such as milking, milling flour, spinning thread, and weaving. I also saw many wedding customs from traditional Tibetan culture. These customs carry rich ethnic culture and also show the spirit of our time. I pointed my camera at this Amdo Tibetan wedding to show the rich life of Tibetan people, which joins tradition and modern life. In a small way, I also show how bright ethnic traditional culture is in the new era.
