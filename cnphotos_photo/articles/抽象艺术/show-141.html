The 4th “Aerial View” National Aerial Photography Competition of 2023 was hosted by China Photo Network. It called for entries for more than four months. It received 78,463 photos (series) and 437 short videos from 7,933 photographers. The photographers came from 31 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities. They also came from Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan in China, and from countries such as Singapore, Australia, Malaysia, and New Zealand.

After the call closed, the judges held a first review. They selected about 10 percent of the entries for the next round. In the end, 7,810 photos (series) and 172 short videos entered the final review. The final review used a selection rate of about 1.2 percent, close to past editions. It awarded level prizes and excellence awards to 1,020 photos (series) and 50 short videos across five categories: Urban Landscape, Nature, Humanistic Documentary, Abstract Art, and Short Video. The proposed winning works and the name list were shown for one week on all China Photo Network platforms, from August 27 to September 2, 2024.

During the public notice, the public and many photographers oversaw the results. The review committee took reports of rule violations seriously and checked them carefully. After review, it disqualified 6 works. The main problems fell into these groups. First, the submitted works had no EXIF data, and the authors did not provide the original files during the raw adjustment period. Second, the works were shot with a DSLR camera. They showed no clear bird’s-eye view, and the authors could not provide proof that an aircraft assisted the shoot. Third, for Humanistic Documentary works, the news value of an image weighs more than its form and image quality. Works that broke factual truth, such as composites from multiple frames or added and removed scene elements, also lost their award.

We now formally publish the final winning list of the 4th “Aerial View” National Aerial Photography Competition 2023. The winning works will soon be shown online on China Photo Network and on the platforms of co-organizing media. Please follow the updates.
