Announcement: Winning Works of the 2nd "Aerial Vision" National Aerial Photography Contest 2021 — Abstract Art Category

Published: 2022-11-21 | Category: Abstract Art | Source: https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-70.html

        Hosted by China Photo Network, and co-organized by People's Photography, Xinlan.com, the Entrepreneurs Photographers Association (Shenzhen), the Henan Aerial Photography Association, the Hebei Aerial Photography Society, the Anhui Aerial Flight Team, the Tianjin Culture and Tourism Photography Association, the Zhejiang Culture and Tourism Photography Association, the Liaoning Tourism Photography Association, the Jiangxi Tourism Photography Association, the Shaanxi Tourism Photography Association, the Fujian Tourism Photography Association, the Hong Kong-Shenzhen-Zhuhai-Macau Photography Alliance, and the Hangzhou Photographers Association Aerial Branch, the 2nd "Aerial Vision" National Aerial Photography Contest 2021 called for entries for more than four months. It received 53,487 photographs (in series) and 516 short videos from 6,479 photographers. These came from 31 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities, from Hong Kong and Taiwan, and from Singapore, Australia, the United States, and Japan. After the deadline, the works went through a first review at an acceptance rate of about 10%. Then 4,950 photographs (in series) and 115 short videos entered the final review. In the end, at an acceptance rate of about 1.2%, the judges chose 650 photographs (in series) and 49 short videos for merit and excellence awards across five categories: Urban Landscape, Nature, Humanistic Documentary, Abstract Art, and Short Video.

        Under the rules of the call for entries, and in line with fairness, justice, and openness, the proposed winning works were shown on all China Photo Network platforms for one week, from November 22 to 29, 2022.

        The final results will be announced after the public notice period. Later, a book of winning works from the 2nd "Aerial Vision" National Aerial Photography Contest 2021 will be published. An awards ceremony will be held when the pandemic allows. At the same time, the 3rd "Aerial Vision" National Aerial Photography Contest will begin.

        During the public notice period, the public and many photographers oversaw the results. The review committee took reports of rule violations seriously and checked them carefully. After review, it disqualified 5 works. The main problems were these. First, some works had no EXIF data, and the authors did not provide original images during the check. Second, some works were taken with a DSLR camera, had no clear bird's-eye view, and could not show proof of aircraft-assisted shooting. Third, for Humanistic Documentary works, the news value of the image weighs more than its form and picture quality. Works that broke authenticity, such as combining multiple frames or adding and removing scene elements, were also disqualified.

        Now we formally announce the final winning list of the 2nd "Aerial Vision" National Aerial Photography Contest 2021. A book of winning works will be published later. Because of the pandemic, the awards ceremony cannot be held yet. The awards will be mailed out before the end of the year. The winning works will soon be shown online on China Photo Network and the co-organizing media platforms. Please stay tuned.
