2021 Third Minimalist Photography Awards (Abstract Category): Selected Winning Works

Published: 2022-01-24 | Category: Abstract Art

 

The Minimalist Photography Awards is a non-profit association. It is supported by B&W Minimalist Magazine. Milad Safabakhsh founded it to honor, reward, and show the work of talented photographers around the world, and to introduce them to the professional photography industry.


Milad Safabakhsh, founder and president of the MPA, said the Minimalist Photography Awards is the only foundation that broadly and professionally leads the field of minimalist photography as an art form. It aims to present the best work in minimalism.


China Photo Network learned from the organizers of the third Minimalist Photography Awards that the contest had 12 categories. Well-known people from the photography world served as judges.


Nick Moore - British photography magazine


Paula Tognarelli - Griffin Museum of Photography


Peter Ibsen - Sunday-S Gallery


Craig Whitehead - Street photographer


Milad Safabakhsh – B&W Minimalist Magazine


The contest received more than 3,700 entries from 39 countries. In the end, the Australian photographer Allen Koppe won the title of 2021 Minimalist Photographer and a $2,000 prize.
