Announced: Winning Works of the 3rd 2022 Bird's Eye View National Aerial Photography Contest, Abstract Art Category

Published: 2023-08-25 | Category: Abstract Art

The 3rd 2022 Bird's Eye View National Aerial Photography Contest is hosted by China Photography Network. It is co-organized by People's Photography, New Blue Net, the Entrepreneurs Photography Association (Shenzhen), the Henan Aerial Photography Association, the Hebei Aerial Photography Society, the Anhui Aerial Flight Brigade, the Tianjin Culture and Tourism Photography Association, the Zhejiang Culture and Tourism Photography Association, the Liaoning Tourism Photography Association, the Jiangxi Tourism Photography Association, the Shaanxi Tourism Photography Association, the Fujian Tourism Photography Association, the Hong Kong-Shenzhen-Zhuhai-Macao Photo Society, and the Hangzhou Photographers Association Aerial Branch. After more than four months of open submission, it received 81,436 photos (sets) and 549 short videos from 9,718 photographers across 31 provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities, Hong Kong, Taiwan, and countries such as Singapore, Australia, the United States, and Japan.

After the deadline, entries were first judged at an admission rate of about 10 percent. In the end, 7,920 photos (sets) and 187 short videos moved to the final round. The final round used a pass rate of about 1.2 percent, as in past years. It selected 1,022 award-winning photos (sets) and 58 short videos across five categories: Urban Landscape, Natural Landscape, Humanistic Documentary, Abstract Art, and Short Video.

Under the rules of the call for entries, and on the principles of fairness, justice, and openness, the proposed winning works are now posted for public review. The review period runs from August 25 to August 31, 2023. All sectors of society are welcome to oversee the results. If you disagree with any posted work, send a real-name email during the review period, and give specific clues that can be checked.

The final results will be announced after the review period ends. The winning works album of the 3rd 2022 Bird's Eye View National Aerial Photography Contest will be edited and published. An awards ceremony will be held in October or November, and the 4th Bird's Eye View National Aerial Photography Contest will open at the same time.

During the review period, any author who did not send a large image with the entry should email us the large or original image of the posted proposed work. Name the email with work name + name + phone number. If a work has no EXIF data and the original image was not provided, and it cannot be verified, the award will be cancelled.
