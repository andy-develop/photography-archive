Announcement: Winning Works of the 5th "Aerial Vision" National Aerial Photography Contest 2024 — Abstract Art Category

Published: 2025-12-05 | Category: Abstract Art

During the public notice period, people from all parts of society and many photographers took part in oversight. This gave important support to a fair and just contest. The review committee took reports of rule violations very seriously. It checked and reviewed each case one by one.

After strict checking, the contest disqualified 5 works. The violations fell into three main areas. First, some entries had no EXIF data, and the authors could not provide the original images when asked. This did not meet the authenticity rules. Second, some works were taken with a DSLR camera. They lacked the bird's-eye view that aerial work requires, and the authors could not show proof of aircraft-assisted shooting. This did not meet the core rule of an "aerial" contest. Third, some works were shot at a height above the allowed flight limit, and the authors could not provide valid flight filing data. This suggested a possible flight violation and did not meet the contest's safety rules.

The contest committee has always held to the principles of fairness, justice, and openness. It takes the rules of the contest and the truth of the works very seriously. This action protects the credibility of the contest and the rights of the authors. It keeps the professional quality and good name of the contest. We thank the public for its oversight and support. We will continue the work on the winning works and present a high-quality aerial art show.
