Classic Black-and-White Photographs From Abroad: How Many Have You Seen?

Published: 2022-03-12 | Category: Traditional Culture | Source:https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-34.html

On a photographer's palette, black and white is without doubt the most elegant choice. It clears away the noise of symbols. It does not force you to change or to express. Above all, the world changes in an instant, but black and white stays silent forever. Black and white, simple to the extreme, always has a refined feel and an emotional power that color cannot match. The longer you look, the richer and more classic it seems.
