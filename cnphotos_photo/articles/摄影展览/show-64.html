Winners Announced: First China Ningxia (Dawukou) Industrial Culture Photography Exhibition

Published: 2022-10-26 | Category: Photography Exhibition

The First China Ningxia (Dawukou) Industrial Culture Photography Exhibition opened on July 19, 2022. Submissions closed on September 10. The organizers received more than 15,000 works from photographers across 31 provinces and cities. The total number of photos exceeded 30,000. The results were shown online from October 25 to October 31, and closed at 24:00 on the last day. No objections were received. The winning results are now announced.

This exhibition was guided by the Culture and Tourism Department of Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region and the Federation of Literary and Art Circles of Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region. It received support from the Zhejiang Photographers Association. It was hosted by the Dawukou District Committee of the Communist Party of China, the Dawukou District People's Government, the Ningxia Photographers Association, and China Photo Network. It was organized by the Dawukou District Culture, Tourism, Radio and Television Bureau. It had four groups: Industrial and Humanistic, Natural Landscape, Rural Culture, and Short Video.
