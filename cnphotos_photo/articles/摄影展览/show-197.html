Final Results Announced: Selected Works of the 3rd Industrial Culture Photography Exhibition 2025

Published: 2026-01-26 | Category: Photography Exhibition

The light of industrial civilization is fixed forever by the lens. The striving of Chinese manufacturing gathers into strength in images. With the active response of enthusiasts and professional photographers, the 3rd Industrial Culture Photography Exhibition 2025 has closed successfully. It received 8,733 works (series) from 1,708 photographers across 31 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities, and Hong Kong and Macao. With a unique visual language, the makers capture small details of industrial production, fix the moments of change from traditional manufacturing to smart creation, give cold steel warmth, and give the motion of machinery rhythm.

To keep the exhibition professional and fair, the organizing committee followed the principles of fairness, openness, and justice, and a panel of experts ran careful rounds of initial and final review. The proposed selected works, which join thought, art, and industrial character, were shown for public notice from January 28 to February 3, 2026, and were open to public oversight and feedback, to keep the result sound and authoritative. After the notice period, with no valid objection, the final selected list is confirmed.

The selected works cover the grandeur of heavy industry, the precision of smart manufacturing, and the focus of industrial workers. Each image carries the collective memory of industrial growth, and each frame speaks of the dialogue between people and machines and the meeting of technology and humanity. They show photographic art at work and witness the high-quality development of Chinese industry, carry on the spirit of industrial culture, and show the depth and drive of Made in China.
