The Zhejiang Provincial Photography Art Exhibition is held by the Zhejiang Photographers Associationthe longest-running, most widely influential, and largestprovincial photography exhibition. It has run for 18 editions. It has produced many fine works and discovered many photographers. It shows the full achievement and scope of contemporary photography in Zhejiang.

The 19th Zhejiang Provincial Photography Art Exhibition(hereinafter this Exhibition), guided by Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, upholds the Two Directions, the Hundred Flowers policy, and the principles of innovation. It calls on photographers to keep pace with the times, put the people at the center, offer fine works to the people, and use high ethics to lead public taste. It contributes photography to the Eight-Eight Strategy, to building an important window, to becoming a leading province of socialist modernization, and to building a demonstration zone for common prosperity.

This Exhibition was hosted by the Zhejiang Federation of Literary and Art Circles, the Zhejiang Photographers Association, and the Publicity Department of the Lishui Municipal Party Committee. It was organized by the Lishui Federation of Literary and Art Circles, the Lishui Photographers Association, and the Lishui Photography Museum. It was co-organized by the Liandu District Federation of Literary and Art Circles in Lishui. The show opened for entries on May 1, 2022. The deadline was August 8. The closed judging took place on September 26 and 27. The public notice ran online from October 11 to 18. The results were announced on October 24. The exhibition opens in Lishui on November 18.

We look forward to meeting you!
