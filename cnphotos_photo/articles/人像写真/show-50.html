For many people, the first impression of Vietnamese women is that they are beautiful and gentle. This feature shares images of Vietnamese women through the lens of Vietnamese photographer Do Quoc Dinh. Besides beauty, they also show charm and classical grace.

Do Quoc Dinh is skilled in portrait, urban landscape, animal, wildlife, and creative photography. He creates unusual fantasy and surrealist work.

Here you can see fine photographs taken by this master in a macro style. Macro photography takes very close images of small subjects. The beautiful, delicate flowers in his photos show their beauty and grace.

Fresh flowers have always been seen as a true expression of beauty in this world. Do Quoc Dinh does not only try to photograph this flower or that flower in high resolution. He also gives each photo a special glow, mood, and subtle atmosphere. This makes them romantic and mysterious.
