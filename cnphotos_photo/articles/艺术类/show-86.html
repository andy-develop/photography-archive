He is a member of the Architectural Photography Committee of the Architectural Society of China and a teacher at the Zhongyi Imaging School. He has long created photography on architecture and related subjects.

Works:

Set of 30, Urban Cultural Landmarks

Chinese architecture in a new era. In the 21st century, Chinese architecture has developed greatly, and this leap has driven the development of architectural photography. Excellent architects from many countries came to China to practice, pushing Chinese architecture forward. Many local architects also broke the old rules, replacing the linear with the non-linear, blending buildings with nature, and making musical elements more vivid. For example, the Harbin Grand Theatre and the Phoenix International Media Center won international architecture awards and represent a world-class standard. The emotion of art and the abstract thought of numbers have a bond that cannot be broken: the numbers in architecture, the numbers in music, and the numbers in photography. between them forms a harmonious bond. Music, as the soul of life, became the driving force behind my architectural photography. I use a Four-All shooting method: all focal lengths, all angles, all weather, and all process, to support artistic expression in photography. All focal lengths is the choice of lens and style. All angles is the framing and the viewpoint. All weather is the handling of different light. All process is the unity of shooting and post. The 30 photos are shown in three groups: Opera House, Architectural Score, and Urban Cultural Landmarks. Opera House: 14 images. Architecture, as a container of sound, coexists almost in step with music. Opera houses and concert halls best show this bond. The National Centre for the Performing Arts is a landmark building, and its appearance greatly influenced later development. Its three halls are set inside a round sphere, like an alien flying saucer. Some once felt it did not fit the Great Hall of the People, but one square and one round shape also fits the idea of traditional Chinese architecture. Architectural Score: 8 images. Music is converted through numbers onto a plane, and the staff of five lines is the best interpretation. It sets a clear orientation. The relation of music, architecture, and image is a relation of point, line, and plane. These points in architecture and image mark a position on a score. One point is like a note, many points form a melodic line, and the plane forms harmony and polyphony. Urban Cultural Landmarks: 8 images. Urban cultural landmarks stand for a nation's strength and development. When you travel to cities in China and the world, the most important subjects are CBD buildings, libraries, opera houses, and concert halls, the marks of a city. Photography is to gaze and observe through a lens. A photographer must have a sharp eye to find the uncommon in the ordinary, to seek perfection in the imperfect, and to find distinctive beauty in common scenery. Photography is the extension of the human eye, an aesthetics of seeing through a lens. You could say that image language itself carries richer and more vivid meaning than symbolized written language.the spiritual meaning it wants to express always goes beyond the surface of the work，This is the charm of photographic art.。
