Photography career:

He is president of the Guilin Photographers Association. His work has long focused on the Li River in Guilin and similar subjects. He has published several solo photo collections.

Works:

A set of 10 images, Wonderful Realm, Empty View (series)

A set of 10 images, Work and Insight (series)

A set of 10 images, New View of Landscape (series)

I quietly observe the new landscapes of Guilin. Liu Yong of the Qing dynasty wrote: "Mountains and water show movement and stillness as viewed from the heart. Kites and fish reveal their nature according to their setting." In landscape photography, I have long sought a way to show the empty, clear, pure, and silent Zen state in which mountains and water depend on each other and reflect each other. Guilin's landscapes are the finest under heaven. General Secretary Xi Jinping has stated clearly: "The Li River belongs not only to the people of Guilin and Guangxi, and the whole of China, but also to the world. It is a treasure shared by all people, and we must all care for it well." The long waters of the Li River and its dreamlike mist and peaks make the whole world long for the symbol of Eastern landscape mood. As a local photographer, I use my own new visual angle to show the world the strange, airy beauty of Guilin, a land like a fairyland. For a "Beautiful China," I present the era's picture of green mountains and clear waters with new ideas and new methods. I start from the heart and use my camera to see a new landscape. So, when I planned these series, I built my visual language and viewpoint around one word: view. The first set, "Wonderful Realm, Empty View," uses a long, clear, airy style to reach the beauty of a scene where the real and the empty give rise to each other. The second set, "Work and Insight," shows people laboring in the landscape, so I can view the relationship between humans and nature. The third set, "New View of Landscape," uses fresh visual symbols to think about and discover new scenery from a new angle, and it shows the result of the meeting between humans and nature. 

(1) Wonderful Realm, Empty View. Artist's statement: "Landscapes are also fine places, and the noble person loves the woods and streams." In this set, my work follows the direction in which mood meets setting, setting joins mood, and heart and object merge. I try to use a set of vertical, freehand landscape images. Through changes in size and between real and empty for running water, sunrise, mist, and a lone boat, I give the frame a strong sense of form. Through rolling peaks and drifting cloud, I fill the empty space with life. I use the visible to show the formless, the limited to show the infinite, and the empty setting to set off the real one. I join what is visible with what is suggested, and I unify the limited concrete image with the rich images of the imagination. This merges the real scene with the empty world of metaphor and symbol. It forms an empty, full, and restrained mood space, so that I reach the state in which object and self are forgotten, and people merge with all things in heaven and earth. This is the wonderful landscape in which the real and the empty give rise to each other.

(2) Work and Insight. Artist's statement: While I photographed the airy, wonderful landscapes, I found that the Guilin mountains and rivers, worn by wind and rain and stripped of ornament, have another plain, elegant beauty. So I often walked through the fields and along both banks of the Li River. I searched where the sky and water meet, looking for workers in the landscape. I let them become the scenery in my frame, and let their figures form poetic images. Once, twice, three times. One photo, two, three. From stranger to familiar, from familiar to devotion. The workers among the mountains and rivers strengthened my confidence to shoot. Labor creates beauty. Labor creates greatness. Labor creates the landscape. Workers in the landscape are my reflection on the traditional images of Guilin. They are also my search to photograph a different Guilin, so that more photographers can see the rich human scenes within Guilin's extreme natural scenery. Workers in the landscape show the harmony between people and nature in Guilin, and the yearning for it. They also make more people realize how important a healthy natural world is to human life itself. In these images, a traditional beauty and poetry express Guilin's long farming civilization. They also open an experimental field for photography, and in the dialogue between people and nature, they photograph a newer and more beautiful picture of the times.

(3) New View of Landscape. Artist's statement: Where the heart is, the view is. The times change, society moves forward, and Guilin changes day by day with a new face. Guilin's landscape should be a new landscape viewed from the heart. It is also the contemporary new landscape I have long and diligently searched for in recent years by "viewing things from the heart." Starting from this, I have worked for years to view Guilin's mountains and rivers from the heart. From the choice of visual symbols to the use of photographic language, I hope to build and create a "Guilin landscape" in a wholly new way, unlike those before. This is also my reflection on the traditional images of Guilin. "The meaning beyond words, the realm beyond images." The images in this set record how visual symbols and the nature of Guilin depend on each other. They grasp the shifting pulse that human development gives to nature, and they feel the space of imagination beyond the mountains and rivers themselves.
