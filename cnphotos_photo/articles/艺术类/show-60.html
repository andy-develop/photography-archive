Selected Works of the 19th Zhejiang Provincial Photography Art Exhibition: Fine Art

Published: 2022-10-11 | Category: Fine Art

The Zhejiang Provincial Photography Art Exhibition is the oldest, most influential, and largest provincial photography exhibition run by the Zhejiang Photographers Association. It has been held successfully 18 times. It has produced a large body of fine work, discovered many photographic talents, and fully shown the achievements and overall state of contemporary photography in Zhejiang.

The 19th Zhejiang Provincial Photography Art Exhibition (the Provincial Exhibition) is guided by Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era. It upholds the Two Servings direction, the Hundred Flowers and Hundred Schools policy, and the Two Creativity principles. It calls on photographers to keep pace with the times, stay centered on the people, offer fine work to the people, and use virtue to lead social trends. It contributes photographic effort to faithfully carrying out the Eight-Eight Strategy, building an important window, striving to be a pioneer province of socialist modernization, and developing a demonstration zone for common prosperity.

This Provincial Exhibition is hosted by the Zhejiang Federation of Literary and Art Circles, the Zhejiang Photographers Association, and the Publicity Department of the Lishui Municipal Committee of the CPC. It is organized by the Lishui Federation of Literary and Art Circles, the Lishui Photographers Association, and the Lishui Photography Museum, and co-organized by the Liandu District Federation of Literary and Art Circles in Lishui. The exhibition opened for entries on May 1, 2022, and closed on August 8. Closed judging took place on September 26 and 27. Results were published online from October 11 to 18, and the final results were announced on October 24. It opens in Lishui on November 18.
