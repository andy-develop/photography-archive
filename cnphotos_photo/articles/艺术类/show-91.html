Series of 8 photos: Daily Life of People on the Road to Shared Prosperity --- High-Speed Rail Travel

High-speed rail shows "China's speed." Since China's first high-speed rail line opened, the network reached 37,900 km by the end of 2020. That is 70% of the world's total high-speed rail mileage. The "four vertical and four horizontal" network is complete, and the "eight vertical and eight horizontal" network is taking shape. China has built the largest and most modern high-speed rail network in the world. It carries a "green beauty." The trains speed past a beautiful land. High-speed rail uses little energy and carries large loads, so it is itself a model of energy saving. From nothing, through learning and improving on foreign designs, to its own innovation, Chinese high-speed rail now leads the world and overlooks the mountains and rivers. This series shows people riding high-speed rail. It recreates the process of entering the station, buying tickets, security check, waiting, ticket check, and boarding. It shows the great changes that the high-speed rail era has brought to work and daily life in China. The series uses many scenes and people, rebuilt into a new form, to show the real scene of rail travel today.

Series of 10 photos: Daily Life of People on the Road to Shared Prosperity --- The Bustling Fishing Port

The ocean is the cradle of life. It gives birth to life and feeds countless lives with its wide riches. Wenling lies on the coast of southeast Zhejiang, south of Taizhou Bay. The sea wraps around three sides, and the city has 316 km of coastline. It has rich fishery resources and is a paradise for photographers. When the tide goes out, the sunlit mudflats look charming, as if dressed in gold. The fishing harbor is wide, quiet, and warm. The sea glints with small golden waves, and the whole place takes on a fairy-tale color. As boats heavy with catch return to the dock, the harbor comes alive. Busy figures are everywhere. The men who went to sea and the women who stayed on shore all feel the joy of the harvest. Happy smiles spread on every face. They carry basket after basket of heavy seafood from the boats and load it onto trucks. This is the harvest work scene I know well at the Wenling fishing harbor. These hardworking fishers are the most ordinary people. They are plain and change their lives through hard work. Broad shoulders, rough hands, dark faces. Strength and softness meet here in a perfect, real way. They are like my parents, or my brothers and sisters. They give me inner peace and emotion. In post-processing, I added an oil-painting texture. It shows the plain quality and simple daily life of working people under shared prosperity.
