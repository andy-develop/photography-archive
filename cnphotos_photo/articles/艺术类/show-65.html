It was hosted by the Zhejiang Buddhist Association and the Zhejiang Photographers Association, co-organized by the Hangzhou Buddhist Association and the Hangzhou Photographers Association, and run by Hangzhou Lingyin Temple and the Media Center of the Zhejiang Federation of Literary and Art Circles. The call opened on April 28, 2022 and closed on September 15. It received 8,187 works (14,174 photos) from more than 2,000 people across the country. Closed judging ran on September 24 and 25. The results were shown online from October 26 to November 2, and the final results were announced on November 3.
