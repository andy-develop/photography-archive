Announced: Winning Works of the 4th Aerial View National Aerial Photography Competition, 2023, Humanistic Documentary

Published: 2024-08-22 | Category: Humanistic Documentary

The 4th "Aerial View" National Aerial Photography Competition 2023 was hosted by China Photo Network. After more than four months of collecting entries, it received works from 7,933 photographers. They came from 31 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities, from Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan, and from countries such as Singapore, Australia, Malaysia, and New Zealand. They submitted 78,463 photographs (series) and 437 short videos.

After the deadline, the judges held a first round. They selected about 10 percent of the entries. In the end, 7,810 photographs (series) and 172 short videos entered the final round. The final selection used a rate of about 1.2 percent, as in past years. It chose 1,020 award-winning photographs (series) and 50 short videos across five categories: Urban Landscape, Nature, Humanistic Documentary, Abstract Art, and Short Video. The proposed winners and the full list were shown on China Photo Network platforms for one week, from August 27 to September 2, 2024.

During the public notice period, the public and many photographers kept an effective watch on the results. The judging committee took reported rule violations seriously and checked each case carefully. After review, it removed the awards of 6 works. The main problems were as follows. First, the submitted works had no EXIF data, and the authors did not provide the original files during the file check. Second, the works were shot with a DSLR camera. They had no clear bird's-eye view, and the authors could not provide proof that an aircraft helped with the shot. Third, for Humanistic Documentary works, the news value of the image counts more than its form and technical quality. Works that combined multiple originals, or added or removed elements from the scene, also lost their awards because they broke the rule of authenticity.

The final winning list of the 4th "Aerial View" National Aerial Photography Competition 2023 is now officially announced. The winning works will be shown online soon on China Photo Network and its partner media platforms. Please stay tuned!
