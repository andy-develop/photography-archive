        Hosted by China Photo Network, and co-organized by People's Photography, New Blue Network, the Entrepreneur Photographers Association (Shenzhen), the Henan Aerial Photography Association, the Hebei Aerial Photography Society, the Anhui Aerial Flight Brigade, the Tianjin Culture and Tourism Photography Association, the Zhejiang Culture and Tourism Photography Association, the Liaoning Tourism Photography Association, the Jiangxi Tourism Photography Association, the Shaanxi Tourism Photography Association, the Fujian Tourism Photography Association, the Hong Kong-Shenzhen-Zhuhai-Macao Photography Alliance, and the Aerial Branch of the Hangzhou Photographers Association, the 2nd National Aerial Photography Contest Bird's-Eye Vision 2021 ended its call for entries after more than four months. It received 53,487 photos (series) and 516 short videos. These were submitted by 6,479 photographers from 31 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities, from Hong Kong and Taiwan of China, and from Singapore, Australia, the United States, and Japan. After the call closed, a first review selected entries at a rate of about 10 percent. In total, 4,950 photos (series) and 115 short videos entered the final review. The final review used an acceptance rate of about 1.2 percent, the same as past contests. It selected 650 photos (series) and 49 short videos as graded awards and excellence awards across five categories: Urban Landscape, Nature, Humanistic Documentary, Abstract Art, and Short Video.

        Under the contest rules, and on the principles of fairness, justice, and openness, the proposed award works were shown to the public from November 22 to 29, 2022, on all China Photo Network platforms for one week.

        The final results will be published after the public notice period ends. The organizers will then publish the book Award-winning Works of the 2nd National Aerial Photography Contest Bird's-Eye Vision 2021. Depending on the state of the pandemic, they will hold an awards ceremony in due course. They will also launch the 3rd National Aerial Photography Contest Bird's-Eye Vision.

        During the public notice period, the public and photographers supervised the selection. The review committee took reported violations seriously and checked each case carefully. After review, it canceled the award status of five works. The main problems fell into these areas. First, the submitted work had no EXIF data, and the photographer did not provide the original file during the review. Second, the work was shot with a DSLR camera, it had no clear bird's-eye view, and the photographer could not provide proof of assistance from an aircraft. Third, for Humanistic Documentary works, the news value of the image matters more than its form and technical quality. Works that combined multiple frames or added and removed scene elements, in a way that broke authenticity, also lost their award status.

The organizers will then publish the book Award-winning Works of the 2nd National Aerial Photography Contest Bird's-Eye Vision 2021. Because of the pandemic, the awards ceremony cannot be held now. The awards will be mailed before the end of the year. The award works will be shown online soon on China Photo Network and the co-organizing media platforms. Please follow these platforms.
