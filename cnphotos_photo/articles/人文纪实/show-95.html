Announcement | Winners of the 3rd 2022 "Bird's-eye View" National Aerial Photography Contest: Humanistic Documentary

Published: 2023-08-25 | Category: Humanistic Documentary

The 3rd 2022 "Bird's-eye View" National Aerial Photography Contest is hosted by China Photoforum. It is co-organized by People's Photography, New Blue Net, the Entrepreneurs Photographic Association (Shenzhen), the Henan Aerial Photography Association, the Hebei Aerial Photography Society, the Anhui Aerial Flight Brigade, the Tianjin Culture and Tourism Photography Association, the Zhejiang Culture and Tourism Photography Association, the Liaoning Tourism Photography Association, the Jiangxi Tourism Photography Association, the Shaanxi Tourism Photography Association, the Fujian Tourism Photography Association, the Hong Kong-Shenzhen-Zhuhai-Macau Photography Club, and the Hangzhou Photographers Association Aerial Branch. After more than four months of calls for entries, it received 81,436 photos (sets) and 549 short videos. They came from 9,718 photographers in 31 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities, in Hong Kong and Taiwan, and in Singapore, Australia, the United States, and Japan.

After the deadline, a first review selected entries at about 10 percent. In the end, 7,920 photos (sets) and 187 short videos entered the final review. The final review used the past rate of about 1.2 percent. It chose 1,022 photos (sets) and 58 short videos for graded awards and excellence awards across five categories: urban landscape, nature, humanistic documentary, abstract art, and short video.

Under the contest rules, and in line with fairness, justice, and openness, the proposed winners of this contest are now posted for public notice. The notice runs from August 25 to August 31, 2023. All sides are welcome to watch the results. If you object to any posted work, send a real-name email during the notice period, and give specific clues that we can check.

The final results will be released when the notice period ends. We will then edit and publish the book Winners of the 3rd 2022 "Bird's-eye View" National Aerial Photography Contest. We will also hold an award ceremony in October or November, and start the 4th "Bird's-eye View" National Aerial Photography Contest.

During the notice period, authors who did not send a large image with their entry must email the large or original image of their posted work. Use the subject line "work name + name + phone number". If a work has no EXIF data and the author cannot provide the original, we cannot verify it, and we will remove it from the winners.
