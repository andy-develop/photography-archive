The Russian Ministry of Culture has published a set of color photos of China from 1949 to 1950. More than 1,000 of these historical photos have now been sorted out. They were taken by Vladislav Mikosha, a photographer from the former Soviet Union, and they preserve many precious moments of the newly born People's Republic of China.

Many of these photos were shot on color film, which makes them especially precious.

On the morning of October 1, 1949, Mao Zedong, Zhou Enlai, and other party and state leaders ascended the Tiananmen Gate tower. At this solemn, historic moment, a Soviet photographer captured their expressions at close range.

According to records, in September 1949, at the invitation of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China, the Soviet Union sent a film crew to China to help shoot a documentary about the People's Liberation Army and the new life in the liberated areas. The Soviet photographer Vladislav Mikosha was sent to China as a member of the crew. He took part in the documentary "The Triumph of the Chinese People" and, in the same period, shot a large number of color photos that drew wide attention.
