How Beautiful Is the Forbidden City in the Snow?

Published: 2021-11-18 | Category: Famous Landmarks | Source:https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-22.html

Under a cold wave, snow fell across much of the north today,

and, in a single night, the handover from autumn to winter was complete.

The Forbidden City, after a night of wind and snow, put on its winter clothes a little earlier than in past years.


The snow that swirled by the Meridian Gate last night, swirling past the beaded curtain and over the white wall, lighter than catkins yet heavier than frost.


The Golden River has not frozen, but the jade butterflies have arrived first.


The photographers set out early this morning. Follow them to see the beauty of the Forbidden City after the snow.


Red walls, white snow, and glazed tiles, the Forbidden City in the snow is as beautiful as a painting.


The Palace Museum becomes the Forbidden City again. One fall of snow is enough.


Fair towers and jade halls, and the bronze lions and ridge beasts all covered in white snow tell you, in a quiet, stately beauty, that the 600-year-old Forbidden City has never changed.
