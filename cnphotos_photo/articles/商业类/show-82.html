Photographs:

Series of 6: People Wearing Masks

Selected for the 28th National Exhibition. Even when masks cover our faces, they cannot hide the full range of human life. COVID-19 changed the world and our lives. Wearing masks and washing hands became habits. On May 6, 2020, the 100th day that masks became part of our lives, I led my team to mark the change with this series. The mask is the key creative element. Different drinks stand for different people, and different colors stand for different states of life. The images draw on cola, orange juice, apple drink, milk, and cocktails. Using images abstracted from life and the mask as the link, the series tells small stories of real people.

Series of 4: To Youth

I turn my camera to people who work in every part of society: doctors, firefighters, delivery riders, community volunteers, road workers. I photograph these everyday roles. They give their youth quietly to society, turning their best years and passion into steadfastness. I want to write their image with the lens and record the weight of this generation in a single moment. I feel the art of life and use craft to restore simple greatness.

Series of 4: Motion Frames

I place many similar sports photos side by side. Through the quiet dialogue between them, they abstract into single symbols of motion. The series shows the beauty of sport. It also brings out the athletes grit and fighting spirit.

Series of 6: Elegant Force

The 32nd Olympics, set for 2020, had to be delayed because of COVID-19. As a photographer who works closely with athletes, I know well what the delay means for their training. So I picked up the camera to cheer the athletes on their way. I wanted to photograph sports through fashion. This work mixes elements of fashion and sport to create a clash of cultures. I choose different sports, pull out their core elements, and rebuild the scenes with a fashion eye. My goal is to let more people feel the pull of sport through the language of fashion. When sport moves off the field into everyday settings, and when the athletes are no longer sweaty champions but carefully styled fashion leaders, the clash gives the images stronger impact.
