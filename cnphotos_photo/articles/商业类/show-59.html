19th Zhejiang Provincial Photography Art Exhibition Selected Works: Commercial Category

Published: 2022-10-11 | Category: Commercial

The Zhejiang Provincial Photography Art Exhibition is held by the Zhejiang Provincial Photographers Association. It is the province-wide photography exhibition with the longest history, widest influence, and largest scale. It has run successfully for 18 editions. It has produced many fine works and discovered many photographic talents. It fully shows the achievements and overall face of photography in Zhejiang today.

The 19th Zhejiang Provincial Photography Art Exhibition (hereafter the Provincial Exhibition) takes Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era as its guide. It upholds the Two Directions, the Double Hundred policy, and the Two Creations principles. It calls on photographers to keep pace with the times, stay centered on the people, offer fine work to the people, and use sound values to lead public taste. It contributes photographic strength to the faithful practice of the Eight-Eight Strategy, to building an important window, to striving for a leading province in socialist modernization, and to building a demonstration zone for common prosperity through high-quality development.

This Provincial Exhibition is hosted by the Zhejiang Federation of Literary and Art Circles, the Zhejiang Provincial Photographers Association, and the Publicity Department of the Lishui Municipal Party Committee, and organized by the Lishui Federation of Literary and Art Circles, the Lishui Photographers Association, and the Lishui Photography Museum, and co-organized by the Liandu District Federation of Literary and Art Circles of Lishui. The exhibition opened for entries on May 1, 2022, and closed on August 8. Closed judging ran from September 26 to 27, The results were shown online from October 11 to 18. The final results were announced on October 24. The exhibition opens in Lishui on November 18.

We look forward to meeting you!
