Works:

Set of 5, Marunqi Landscape Series

In June 2021, the general manager of Marunqi and I talked about giving the new products a different look. I searched and visited many spaces, but few could support the character of Marunqi home goods. Then I thought of the Juemin Art Museum, which I photographed in 2018. When I first saw the building, I was stunned. I admired Mr. Du's clever design. So I had the idea of using contrast to present contemporary home goods. With Mr. Du's permission, our team planned the shoot and the display in detail. Then, through clearing the space, building sets, arranging the light, finding the composition, and fine retouching, this small album came together.

Set of 4, Marunqi Arrival Series

This is the showroom the Marunqi brand built in 2022, themed on the film Arrival. The round ceiling design looks like a space capsule, with a strong sci-fi feel. In the images I used low cool tones to set off its mystery and future feel, and added orange and warm accents here and there, so the cool and warm tones echo each other.

Set of 4, Tingshi Elegant Luxury Series

The 2022 new Tingshi products blend many elements: metal structures carved from a single block, hand-woven details, and more durable leather upgrades, all showing high quality. For this shoot I chose an indoor setting, and cleverly blended the architectural space with the home goods.

Set of 3, Tingshi Seaview Series

In 2016 I shot Tingshi's first series in Taizhou. After talking with the brand planner, I decided to shoot a series of seaview images. After several site visits and preparation, we carried the home goods over the mountains to the seaside.

Set of 6, Jizhi INFLUXURY

From 2019 to 2022 I kept shooting brand catalogs and ads for Jizhi home goods. Jizhi products are updated every year, and my visual planning is updated too. This set also shows the visual shift from the old deep and low-key style to the now-popular light and pure style.

Set of 7, Dongfanghui Garden Tour Series

Dongfanghui is a modern home brand with strong Eastern aesthetics. From April to August 2019, I combined modern Chinese home goods with landscape, nature, and architecture, to express the charm of Eastern culture and the unique character of modern Chinese home goods.
