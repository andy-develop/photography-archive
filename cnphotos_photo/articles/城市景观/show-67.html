Posted | 2021Awarded Works of the 2nd Aerial View National Aerial Photography Competition: Urban Landscape Category

Date: 2022-11-21 ｜ Category: Urban Landscape


        Hosted by China Photography Net, co-organized by People Photography, New Blue Net, the Entrepreneur Photographers Association (Shenzhen), the Henan Aerial Photography Association, the Hebei Aerial Photography Society, the Anhui Aerial Flight Team, the Tianjin, Zhejiang, Liaoning, Jiangxi, Shaanxi, and Fujian cultural tourism photography associations, the HK-Shenzhen-Zhuhai-Macau photography alliance, and the Hangzhou Photographers Association aerial branch, the 2021 2nd Aerial View national aerial photography competition received submissions over 4 months, from photographers in 31 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities, Hong Kong, and Taiwan, as well as Singapore, Australia, the United States, Japan, and other regions6479 , who submitted 53487 photo works (sets) and 516 short videos. After the deadline, entries were pre-selected at about 10% , and 4950 photo works (sets) and 115 short videos reached the final. At a final selection rate near 1.2% of earlier years, the judges awarded prizes across Urban Landscape, Nature, Humanistic Documentary, Abstract Art, and Short Video, in 5 categories, giving 650 photo works (sets) and 49 works.

        Under the rules, on the principles of fairness, justice, and openness, the proposed winners were publicized from 20221122 to 29 on China Photography Net platforms for one week.

        The final results will be announced after the publicity period. A book of winning works, 2021 2nd Aerial View National Aerial Photography Competition Collection, will be published. An awards ceremony will be held as the pandemic allows, and the 3rd Aerial View competition will start.

        During the publicity period, the public and photographers supervised the results. The judges carefully reviewed reported violations. After review, 5 works were disqualified. The main issues were:1、submitted works had no EXIF data, and the original files were not provided when requested;2、works shot with a DSLR without a clear aerial view, and without valid proof of aircraft-assisted shooting;3、for Humanistic Documentary works, news value outweighs form and image quality, and works violating authenticity through compositing or adding or removing scene elements were also disqualified.

        We now formally announce the final winners of the 2021 2nd Aerial View national aerial photography competition. A collection will be published.2021Because of the pandemic, the awards ceremony is on hold, and prizes will be mailed before year end. The winning works will be exhibited online soon on China Photography Net and partner media. Please stay tuned!
