Announcement: Winning Works of the 4th Aerial Vision National Aerial Photography Competition 2023 - Urban Landscape Category

Published: 2024-08-22 | Category: Urban Landscape | Source: https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-138.html

The 4th Aerial Vision National Aerial Photography Competition 2023 was hosted by China Photo Network. After more than four months of collecting entries, it received 78,463 photographs (series) and 437 short videos. These came from 7,933 photographers. The photographers came from 31 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities, from Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan, and from countries such as Singapore, Australia, Malaysia, and New Zealand.

After the deadline, a first round selected works at a rate of about 10 percent. In the end, 7,810 photographs (series) and 172 short videos entered the final round. The final selection used a rate of about 1.2 percent, as in past years. It chose 1,020 photographs (series) and 50 short videos. These works won graded awards or awards of excellence across five categories: Urban Landscape, Nature, Humanistic Documentary, Abstract Art, and Short Video. The shortlisted works and names were posted on China Photo Network platforms for one week, from August 27 to September 2, 2024.

During the notice, the public and photographers oversaw the results. The jury took reported violations seriously and checked each case. After review, 6 works were disqualified. The main problems were these: 1. The submitted work had no EXIF data, and the author did not provide the original during the technical check. 2. The work was shot with a DSLR, showed no clear aerial view, and had no proof that an aircraft helped. 3. For Humanistic Documentary works, news value matters more than form and image quality. Works that merged multiple frames or added or removed scene elements, against authenticity, were also disqualified.

The final winners of the 4th Aerial Vision National Aerial Photography Competition 2023 are now official. The winning works will be shown online soon on China Photo Network and partner media. Please stay tuned.
