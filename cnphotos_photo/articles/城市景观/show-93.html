The 3rd Aerial Vision National Aerial Photography Competition 2022 was hosted by China Photo Network. They also came from Hong Kong and Taiwan, and from countries such as Singapore, Australia, the United States, and Japan.

After the deadline, the works went through a first round at a selection rate of about 10 percent. In the end, 7,920 photographs (series) and 187 short videos entered the final round. The final selection used a rate of about 1.2 percent, as in past years. It chose 1,022 photographs (series) and 58 short videos. These works won graded awards or awards of excellence across five categories: Urban Landscape, Nature, Humanistic Documentary, Abstract Art, and Short Video.
