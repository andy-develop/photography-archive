Street Photography View: Using the Street as a Mirror to Explore the Self in Light and Shadow

Published: 2025-11-27 | Category: Street Photography | Source:https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-186.html

Ilker Karaman is a Turkish photographer and an electronic engineer. He sees street photography as an ongoing search for himself. His major work, In Search of the Self, was all shot in Ankara, where he lives. He cleverly adds his own shadow or reflection into the frame. This breaks the objective record of traditional street photography and gives the work a strong subjective view and a look at identity.
Karaman uses high-saturation colors, strong contrast, and geometric composition. He especially likes the visual tension that yellow brings. His visual language is clear and easy to recognize. He often shoots in the light from afternoon to dusk. He uses long shadows and sharp light to build the rhythm of the frame. During the shoot, he is both the photographer and the model. He plans the composition and the light in advance, and places himself into the street scene.
For Karaman, photography is not only a form of creation. It is also a kind of healing. He wants his camera to wake people up to the visual beauty they overlook in daily life. He wants them to rediscover the poetry close at hand in a busy life. This search has no end. As he says, the journey to find the self is always under way.
