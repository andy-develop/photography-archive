Marcin Baran did not train as a professional photographer. But his passion and talent for photography have earned him notable success in the field.

Baran's photos use the city street as their main setting. They focus on lonely people and on small details of daily life. His work often shows lonely figures scattered across the city street. These images show the city's unique mood, where noise and loneliness exist side by side. They also reveal the complex, many-sided nature of human beings.
