Kai Hsiao is a photographer from Taiwan who is skilled at using the interplay of light and shadow to create his work. In his images, the use of color stands out. He expresses emotion through color, making each frame more lively and moving. His color combinations blend the soft and the strong, full of a rich mood, and they bring the viewer a strong visual impact.
