Street Photography | The Distinctive Texture of Film Colors

Published: 2024-09-05 | Category: Street Photography | Original: https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-142.html

Mark Forbes loved travel as a child. He especially liked the time he spent in airports and on planes. This drive to explore new things likely shaped his later style of photography.
Forbes uses film as his main way to record life. He shoots with traditional 35mm and medium format cameras. The unique texture and color of film give his work a strong retro feel and an artistic tone.
People rarely appear in his photos. Instead, he uses ordinary objects from daily life, such as an empty soda bottle or a beach umbrella on the grass, to bring back memories and feelings. These common scenes become full of mood and imagination through his lens.
