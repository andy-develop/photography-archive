Street Photography | Alleys and Lanes Through the Lens of a Wandering Photographer

Published: 2024-07-22 | Category: Street Photography | Original: https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-102.html

Jonathan Jasberg is a unique photographer who calls himself a "wanderer." He travels the world. He captures everyday moments with rare skill, creativity, and a strong eye for composition.

"I know that a unique moment happens all the time. If I do not have a camera in my hand and a curious eye, I will never find it."- Jonathan Jasberg
