In an age when digital technology has spread across the world, Jerry Uelsmann remains an alchemist in the darkroom. He does not use computer compositing. Instead, he uses several enlargers to print different negatives on top of each other. By hand, he builds a poetic surreal world. Bodies float. Trees grow hands. Houses drift on clouds. These images come from fragments of reality, yet they go far beyond the logic of reality. He shows us that real creation is not in the tool itself. It is in the free reach of the imagination. Even today, when everyone can press the shutter, Uelsmann reminds us that photography is a visual meditation that grows from within.
