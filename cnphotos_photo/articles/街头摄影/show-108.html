Street Photography View | Light and Shadow Intertwined, an Art Journey

Published: 2024-07-23 | Category: Street Photography

Amid the interplay of light and shadow, there is a photographer who catches the eternity of a moment. He is Kai Hsiao, from Taiwan. Each frame shows his fine feeling for the world and his strong pursuit of the art of light.

Kai Hsiao's work is a feast of light and shadow. He is good at finding fleeting beauty in a busy city or a quiet foreign place. Through careful composition and color, he makes the ordinary become special. As light and shadow cross, the viewer feels emotion moving and a story flowing.
