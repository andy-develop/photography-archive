Sergey Maximishin is a noted photographer and journalist from Russia. He was born in 1964. His style is mainly documentary. He uses his camera to catch real moments in life, especially ordinary scenes that are also out of the ordinary.
Sergey Maximishin won the top prize at the World Press Photo contest twice. His winning work earned wide praise for its deep themes, unique viewpoint, and skilled technique.
