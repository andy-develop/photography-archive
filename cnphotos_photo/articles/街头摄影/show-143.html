Street Photography View | A Unique Point of View

Published: 2024-09-05 | Category: Street Photography

The work of photographer Sarah van Rij is known for its mystery and novelty. She is good at catching the most ordinary scenes of daily life. Through unique composition, rich color, and varied depth of field (the distance between the sharp and blurry parts of an image), she turns them into gripping stories.
Her work often gives people a surreal kind of harmony. It is like peeking through small holes and gaps into another world.
