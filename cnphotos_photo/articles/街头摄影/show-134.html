Street Photo Vision: The Visual Tension of Black and White

Published: 2024-08-08 | Category: Street Photography | Source: https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-134.html

The Russian metro stations in the work of photographer Dima Zverev. Zverev mostly shoots in black and white, paired with strong contrast of light and shade. This choice raises the contrast and layers of the photos. It also gives the work a unique artistic mood.
He uses light and shadow to bring out the theme and feeling of each photo. This lets viewers feel the message more deeply.
