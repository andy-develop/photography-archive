As a street photographer, Reyhan Turk catches moments in the city with sharp insight and a unique eye. He likes to watch and shoot city scenes from unique angles. Through different ways of composing, he shows the diversity and complexity of the city.
His images often show human silhouettes and building outlines. In his compositions, these elements take on a new life and meaning.
