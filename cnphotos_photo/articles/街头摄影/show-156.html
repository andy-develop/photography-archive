Street View | Using Light as a Brush to Paint the Soul of Urban Space

Published: 2024-09-12 | Category: Street Photography

Clarissa Bonet is a photographic artist based in Chicago. Through a unique view, she deeply explores the physical and psychological sides of urban space. She cleverly uses the city as a canvas, adding people as contrast and reference points, and shows the way people interact with space.

Clarissa Bonet's work is not a simple record of images. It is a deep look at city life. She places people within a large urban landscape and, through contrast and reference, shows the complex and subtle relationship between people and space.
