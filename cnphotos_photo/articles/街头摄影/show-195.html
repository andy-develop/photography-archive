Selected Works of the 2nd Street Photo Vision National Street Photography Festival 2025 (Final Round)

Published: 2025-12-18 | Category: Street Photography | Source: https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-195.html

The 2nd Street Photo Vision National Street Photography Festival 2025 was hosted by China Photo Network. Photographers at home and abroad could enter. There was no age, equipment, or location limit. The theme was street photography only. Only works taken after 2021 were accepted. Each person could enter at most 10 works.

After two months of collecting entries, the festival received 29,722 photographs and short videos. These came from 4,066 photographers. The photographers came from 31 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities, from Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan, and from countries such as Malaysia, Russia, and Australia. After the deadline, three rounds of first review selected 966 works.

After the first review, the team ran a technical check on the original files, suited to street photography. Experts judged the works on authenticity, the decisive moment, storytelling, and art. They also considered the review opinions of the 2025 Lishui Photography Festival. In the end, 519 works were selected for the final show.

Under the rules of the call for entries, and on the principles of fairness, justice, and openness, the shortlisted works are now public. The public notice runs from December 19 to December 25, 2025. Members of the public may oversee the results. If you object to a listed work, send a real-name email during the notice period. Include specific and checkable leads.
During the notice, selected authors who did not send a large file over 5 MB must email a large version with EXIF data to the given address.
