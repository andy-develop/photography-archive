Street View | Rich Color: Rainy Days Through the Lens

Published: 2024-08-06 | Category: Street Photography

The photographer Jake Michaels is a noted artist from Los Angeles, known for his clever use of color, light, and shadow.
He is good at catching the changes in light, shadow, and color and weaving them into his work, presenting images of strong visual impact.
The color in his work is bright and rich, and the light and shadow are clear and strongly contrasted, creating a distinct mood and emotion.
