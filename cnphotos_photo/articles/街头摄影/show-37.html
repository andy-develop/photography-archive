Color on the Street | Constantine Manos

Published: 2022-03-16 | Category: Street Photography

The Magnum photographer Constantine Manos works with rich, saturated colors that feel alive against the beaches and bright sun of the United States. Energetic colors and varied framing come together in the vivid, warm scenes in his frame. People in motion cast their shadows on bright walls, and small moments of joy and delight are held intact.

"The color is so strong!" This is what many people say about his work. His control of story and color in the frame still feels rare. He seems highly sensitive to color. Even the separate colors and light of the street turn into a kind of "novel-like mood" in his hands. It is a color world that is mature and sharp, yet dreamlike.
