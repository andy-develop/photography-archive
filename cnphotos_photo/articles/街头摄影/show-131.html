Street Photography View | Truly Free Only When Alone

Published: 2024-08-08 | Category: Street Photography

The work of photographer Cody Klintworth has the theme of "the night walker alone." It focuses on catching the loneliness and freedom of night.
In his work, a wide scene meets the back view of one person. This creates a mood that is lonely and romantic at once.
He uses the contrast of light and shadow well. He sets the quiet of night against bright light sources, such as street lamps, car lights, and the lights of a city. This makes a unique visual experience. His precise control of light gives his work deep feeling and strong artistic power.
