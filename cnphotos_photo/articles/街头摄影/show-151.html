Street Photo Vision: The Magical Streets of Light and Shadow

Published: 2024-09-12 | Category: Street Photography

Andreas Jorgensen was born in the 1970s and now lives in Sweden. He is an artist who focuses on street photography. He has a sharp, skilled feel for light.

He controls light with great precision. He uses natural and artificial light to create unique effects. In his work, the overlap and contrast of light and shadow become a key visual language. It adds depth and dimension to each frame.
