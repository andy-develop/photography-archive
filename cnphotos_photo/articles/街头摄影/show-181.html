Ilker Karaman’s photography is a perfect blend of reason and poetry on the street. This Turkish electronic engineer captures the strong afternoon contrast of light and shade with the care of someone calculating the angle of light. He builds the inner order of each frame with geometry. In his photos, the yellow walls, round arches, and square windows of Ankara become elements in a visual formula. A figure who walks in by chance, or a sheet fluttering, adds fresh life to that precision. For him, photography is both a spiritual outlet and a light experiment that needs no post-processing. In the open-air laboratory of the city, he shows us that order and chance are both full of poetry.
