Street View | Stockholm Streets in Light and Shadow

Published: 2024-07-29 | Category: Street Photography

Andreas Jorgensen has said, I only shoot when the sun is out. Shadows, silhouettes, and magical light are what I keep looking for in the city, and what I want to feel in them is calm and harmony.

This line reflects not only his love and pursuit of light and shadow, but also reveals his core belief and creative attitude as a photographer.

He keeps to a creative belief: light and shadow as his brush, color as his ink, and emotion as his soul. He keeps exploring and pushing the boundaries of photographic art.
