Street Photography View | Rich Color in Street Photography, Jake Michaels

Published: 2024-11-28 | Category: Street Photography

Jake Michaels is a photographer from Los Angeles. His work uses light and shadow in a unique way. The colors are bold and strong. The composition is careful and designed. Each picture deserves a close look. He uses rich color and the contrast of light and shadow. He turns the image into a palette and makes street pictures with a style of his own.
