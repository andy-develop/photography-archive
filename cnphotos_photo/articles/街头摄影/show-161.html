Miguel Rio Branco is a versatile artist. His work is known for its rich, bold colors. It has an "oppressive" visual power. The saturated colors seem to carry people into a world of another dimension. In that world, everything seems covered with a thick layer of oil paint. It feels strongly like an oil painting, yet it has a vivid sense of the real. He uses color to stir the natural feelings we have long grown used to. Intense, clashing, and full of life, that is the emotion of his color.
