Street View | Dark Tones and Rich Color on the Street

Published: 2024-07-23 | Category: Street Photography | Source:https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-106.html

Dark, rich-color street photography is a style with a unique appeal and strong visual impact. It emphasizes low-saturation, low-brightness color and strong contrast between light and dark. It builds a mood that is mysterious, deep, or vintage, so that people feel a different emotion and story in an ordinary city scene.
