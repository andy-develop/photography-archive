Street Photography View: Finding Light in the Dark Corners

Published: 2025-11-17 | Category: Street Photography | Source:https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-177.html

In the work of Ferdinando Scianna, photography is never a robbery of a moment. It is a gentle way to hold on to time as it passes. He walked the streets after the war, using his camera as a lamp. He watched the daily life of ordinary people in the dark corners and between light and shadow. He did not pursue a grand historical story. Instead, in the small expressions of little people and the quiet instant of an ordinary street scene, he gave the images dignity and poetry. Scianna shows us that true photography is a humble gaze and an honest wait. It is the act of fishing out, in the corners of the world, the light of humanity that is overlooked but truly there.
