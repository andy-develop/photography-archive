Street Photography View: A Unique Way of Seeing

Published: 2024-08-06 | Category: Street Photography

Street photographer Julia Coddington is from Australia. Her work is widely praised for its unique way of seeing and deep insight. Julia Coddington focuses on the skills of staying invisible and blending into the scene. She believes street photography is not only about catching a moment. It is about placing yourself inside the environment and becoming an unseen part of it. In this way, you observe and record more naturally the interaction between people and their surroundings, along with shape, movement, color, light, and shadow.
