Street Vision: Catching Fleeting Moments on the Street

Published: 2024-09-12 | Category: Street Photography

Marius Vieth is a German street photographer who draws wide attention. His work is true and natural. He is good at catching fleeting moments and details on the street. Through his lens, he shows the true face of the city and the way people live.

His work often draws the eye with a unique view and composition. He uses different angles and distances to show the unique character of the city and the many sides of human nature.
