Street Photography View | Color on the Beach

Published: 2024-07-22 | Category: Street Photography

A skilled photographer can work without drawing attention and catch the good moments when different groups interact.

Constantine Manos captured the energy of life on the American coast.

"On the beach, people are relaxed and have fun. They enjoy the sun and the surf, so they ignore the photographer. This holds as long as he moves carefully and never locks onto one group or person for too long."- Constantine Manos
