Street Photo Vision: Order Within Chaos

Published: 2024-08-07 | Category: Street Photography | Source: https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-126.html

The Magnum master Alex Webb has a unique style. His colors are rich but still harmonious, and his frames are complex but ordered.

He is good at catching fleeting moments on the street. Through his lens he records the interaction of people and place, and the shapes, movement, color, light, and shadow.
