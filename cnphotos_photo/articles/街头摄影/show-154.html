Street Photo Vision: Tokyo Street Photography

Published: 2024-09-12 | Category: Street Photography | Source: https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-154.html

Daiki Hosaka is a street photographer. He is good at catching everyday moments in the city. Through his lens he shows the unique pull of the city and the many sides of human nature.

His work is more than a record of reality. It is a deep expression of feeling. Through light, color, and composition, he draws the viewer into the emotional world he creates.
