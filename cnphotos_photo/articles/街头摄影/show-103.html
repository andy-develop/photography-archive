Street Photography | Clever Viewpoints, Striking Composition

Published: 2024-07-22 | Category: Street Photography

Work by the street photographer Lorenzo Catena. Catena is known for catching street moments from non-traditional viewpoints. He takes the viewer into a world that is both familiar and strange. His photos are more than a record. They are an artistic recreation. Through clever choice of viewpoint and careful arrangement, he blends many elements into a harmonious whole. The result is a strong visual impact and an artistic feel.
