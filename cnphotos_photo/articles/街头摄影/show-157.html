The photography of Moises Levy is mostly black and white. Through his unique point of view and his shooting technique, he turns ordinary beach scenes into a whimsical 'land of giants.' He shoots from a low angle. He crouches or lies down. Through the way near objects look large and far objects look small, and through forced perspective (tricking the eye with position), he creates a clear and playful contrast.
