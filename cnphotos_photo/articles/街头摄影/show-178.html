Street Photography View | A Poetic Expedition Home

Published: 2025-11-17 | Category: Street Photography

The camera of Gleeson Paulino is a poetic expedition home. He leaves behind the stereotypes of Brazil. He uses color as his mother tongue. Between the richness of the Amazon and the pulse of the city, he rebuilds a homeland that is both real and dreamlike. His images are strong, fresh, and expressive. Each frame is a gentle confirmation of memory and identity. What we see is not only a portrait of a country. It is also the deep journey of a traveler who reclaims his homeland through vision.
