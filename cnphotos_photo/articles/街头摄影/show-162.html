2024 First Street Frame National Street Photography Exhibition: Final Selections

Published: 2024-12-31 | Category: Street Photography | Source: https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-162.html

Street photography is a genre full of contradictions. It expresses frankly, yet it frames with care. It is unexpected, yet it happens every day.

Chance encounters, rare juxtapositions, and a shutter at the millisecond level reveal the fleeting miracles of daily life.

A changing society slowly seeps into the streets, subways, parks, and arcades. Each place is different from the past.

Strange, wonderful, and at times worrying life unfolds around us.

The 2024 First Street Frame National Street Photography Exhibition was hosted by China Photo Network. Photographers at home and abroad could submit work. There was no age limit, no equipment limit, and no limit on the region photographed. The work had to be about street photography only. Only works shot after 2020 were accepted. Each person could submit at most 10 works.

After two months of collection, the organizers received work from 2,693 photographers. They came from 31 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities, from Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan in China, and from countries and regions such as Malaysia, the United States, Japan, and Singapore. The works included 17,814 photos and series, and 27 short videos. After the deadline, the works went through three rounds of initial review. In the end, 586 photos and series by 345 photographers passed the initial review.

After the initial review, the organizers checked the selected works against the originals for technical issues, in line with the special requirements of street photography. They invited experts and scholars to judge the works on truth, the decisive moment, storytelling, and artistry. They also took into account the review opinions of the 2024 FIAP World Photographic Congress organizing committee. In the end, 256 still photos and series by 207 photographers, and 3 short videos, were selected. The selection rate was 1.4 percent. The final selection list is now officially announced.
