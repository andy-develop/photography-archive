He is not a director, but he makes the city his film set. He does not rely on post-processing, yet he takes stunning visual magic. Jeremy Paige is a Los Angeles street photographer. With a sharp photographic eye, he captures coincidental, humorous, and dramatic moments on the street, weaving ordinary sidewalks, glass facades, and afternoon light into visual poems.
Through his lens, a railing may cut the sky, glass reflections join the real and the unreal, and a pedestrian's shadow overlaps cleverly with an ad. These seemingly accidental frames are really the result of long observation, waiting, and thought. Paige believes that the essence of street photography is to learn to see the hidden visual links in life. He rarely uses complex gear. Instead he looks to natural light and the geometry of the city, telling stories with light and making fun with misdirection.
For any amateur who wants to try street photography, Paige's work is a gentle encouragement. The most beautiful frame is not far away. It is on the street you pass every day. If you slow down and look closely, the next magic moment may come the second after you press the shutter.
