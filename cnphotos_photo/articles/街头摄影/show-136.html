Street Photography View: Focusing on Light and Shadow in the Street

Published: 2024-08-08 | Category: Street Photography

The photographs of street photographer Kai Hsiao mostly focus on city life and street scenes. Through a unique point of view and deep insight, he turns familiar city scenes into images full of artistry and emotional tension. When people view his work, they often feel a sense of loneliness and sadness they cannot name. At the same time, the images are full of love and care for life and the city.
