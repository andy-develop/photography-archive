The old power plant shut down. It stopped in a way that felt sudden.

It is like me. Wow, how did I get to be this old!

I worked on plant maintenance for 18 years. For 8 of those years I lived and worked on a frontline team. The old plant shut down, but it can be reborn in another form. When we retire, we are retired for good.

So I took some photos. For the plant, and for myself.
