Announced: Winning Works of the 3rd Bird's-Eye View National Aerial Photography Competition, 2022 — Nature Category

Published: 2023-08-25 | Category: Nature | Source:https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-94.html

The 3rd Bird's-Eye View National Aerial Photography Competition, 2022, was hosted by China Photo Network and co-organized by the People's Photography newspaper, New Blue TV, the Entrepreneurs Photography Association, Shenzhen, aerial photography associations across the provinces, cultural and tourism photography associations, the Hong Kong-Shenzhen-Zhuhai-Macao photography society, and the aerial branch of the Hangzhou Photographers Association. After more than four months of open submission, it received 81,436 photo series and 549 short videos from 9,718 photographers. They came from 31 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities, from Hong Kong and Taiwan, and from countries such as Singapore, Australia, the United States, and Japan.

After the deadline, the entries were given a first review at an acceptance rate of about 10 percent. In the end, 7,920 photo series and 187 short videos moved to the final review. The final review used the usual acceptance rate of about 1.2 percent. It selected 1,022 photo series and 58 short videos for graded and honorable awards across five categories: urban landscape, nature, humanistic documentary, abstract art, and short video.

Under the rules of the call for entries, and in line with the principles of fairness, justice, and openness, the proposed winners of this competition are now posted for public notice. The notice period runs from August 25 to 31, 2023. People from all sides are welcome to oversee the results. If you have a concern about a posted work, raise it by email under your real name during the notice period, and include verifiable details.

The final results will be announced after the notice period ends. We will then edit and publish an award collection of the 3rd Bird's-Eye View National Aerial Photography Competition, 2022. An awards ceremony will be held in October or November, and the 4th Bird's-Eye View National Aerial Photography Competition will open at the same time.

During the notice period, authors who did not send a large image with their submission should email the large or original file of their proposed winning work to us. Name the subject of the email as work name plus your name plus your phone number. If a work has no EXIF data and the original file is not provided for verification, its award status will be withdrawn.
