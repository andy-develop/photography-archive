Posted | 2023Awarded Works of the 4th Aerial View National Aerial Photography Competition: Nature Category

Date: 2024-08-22 ｜ Category: Nature

Hosted by China Photography Net, the 2023 4th Aerial View national aerial photography competition received submissions over 4 months, from photographers in 31 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities, Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan, as well as Singapore, Australia, Malaysia, New Zealand, and other regions7933 , who submitted 78463 photo works (sets) and 437 short videos.

After the deadline, entries were pre-selected at about 10% , and 7810 photo works (sets) and 172 short videos reached the final. At a final selection rate near 1.2% of earlier years, the judges awarded prizes across Urban Landscape, Nature, Humanistic Documentary, Abstract Art, and Short Video, in 5 categories, giving 1020 photo works (sets) and 50 works. The proposed results were publicized from 2024827 to 92 on China Photography Net platforms for one week.

During the publicity period, the public and photographers supervised the results. The judges carefully reviewed reported violations. After review, 6 works were disqualified. The main issues were:1、submitted works had no EXIF data, and the original files were not provided when requested;2、works shot with a DSLR without a clear aerial view, and without valid proof of aircraft-assisted shooting;3、for Humanistic Documentary works, news value outweighs form and image quality, and works violating authenticity through compositing or adding or removing scene elements were also disqualified.

We now formally announce the final winners of the 2023 4th Aerial View national aerial photography competition. The winning works will be exhibited online soon on China Photography Net and partner media. Please stay tuned!
