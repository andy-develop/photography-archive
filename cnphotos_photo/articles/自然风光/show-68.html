Announcement: Winning Works of the 2nd Aerial View National Aerial Photography Contest 2021, Nature Category

Published: 2022-11-21 | Category: Nature

The 2nd Aerial View National Aerial Photography Contest 2021 was hosted by China Photo Network. It was co-organized by People's Photography, New Blue Net, the Entrepreneurs Photography Association (Shenzhen), the Henan Aerial Photography Association, the Hebei Aerial Photography Society, the Anhui Aerial Flight Brigade, the Tianjin Culture and Tourism Photography Association, the Zhejiang Culture and Tourism Photography Association, the Liaoning Tourism Photography Association, the Jiangxi Tourism Photography Association, the Shaanxi Tourism Photography Association, the Fujian Tourism Photography Association, the Hong Kong-Shenzhen-Zhuhai-Macao Photography Alliance, and the Hangzhou Photographers Association Aerial Branch. After more than four months of call for entries, the contest received work from 6,479 photographers. They came from 31 provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities, from Hong Kong and Taiwan, and from countries and regions such as Singapore, Australia, the United States, and Japan. They submitted 53,487 photographs (in groups) and 516 short videos. After the call closed, the jury held a first review at an acceptance rate of about 10 percent. In this review, 4,950 photographs (in groups) and 115 short videos moved to the final review. The final review used an acceptance rate of about 1.2 percent, based on past contests. It selected 650 photographs (in groups) and 49 short videos for graded awards and honorable mentions across five categories: urban landscape, nature, humanistic documentary, abstract art, and short video.

Under the rules of the call, and on the principles of fairness, justice, and openness, the proposed winning works were posted for public review from November 22 to 29, 2022. The review ran for one week on all China Photo Network platforms.

The final results will be announced after the public review period ends. The winning works of the 2nd Aerial View National Aerial Photography Contest 2021 will later be edited and published as a collected volume. An awards ceremony will be held when the pandemic situation allows. At the same time, the 3rd Aerial View National Aerial Photography Contest will begin.

During the public review, people from all parts of society and many photographers monitored the results. The jury took the reported and flagged violations of certain works seriously and checked them carefully. After review, it stripped the award status from 5 works. The main problems fell into these areas: 1. The submitted work did not carry EXIF data (the camera record stored in the file), and the photographer did not provide the original files during the file review. 2. The work was shot with a single-lens reflex camera. It had no clear aerial view, and the photographer could not provide valid proof of help from an aircraft. 3. For humanistic documentary works, the news value of an image is weighed more than its form and picture quality. Works that violated truthfulness, such as composites from multiple files or added or removed scene elements, also lost their award status.

The final winners of the 2nd Aerial View National Aerial Photography Contest 2021 are now officially announced. The winning works will later be edited and published as a collected volume. Because of the pandemic, the awards ceremony cannot be held for now. The awards will be mailed before the end of the year. The winning works will be shown online soon on China Photo Network and the co-organizing media platforms. Please stay tuned.
