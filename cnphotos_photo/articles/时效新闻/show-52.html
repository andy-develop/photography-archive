Flowing Like Clouds and Water

Published: 2022-09-08 | Category: News

On September 7, 2022, reporters entered the shared smart material preparation center in Nankang District, Ganzhou City, Jiangxi Province. In the smart warehouse of furniture parts, storage robots moved the finished parts to high three-dimensional racks with ease. The whole process needed no human work.

The Nankang Longhui shared smart material preparation center is the first 5G standalone industrial internet factory in the domestic furniture industry. Its rate of automation reaches 95%. The whole line is like a smart highway. It allows wireless production without jams. It also adjusts its wiring at any time based on changes in capacity and customer demand.

Estimates show the shared center uses automated lines and smart manufacturing systems, together with large-scale operation. Compared with existing material lines on the market, it raises efficiency by more than 50% and cuts costs by more than 20%. In addition, the shared model raises coordination across the Nankang furniture supply chain. It increases the flow speed of rubberwood by 15% and raises the overall wood cutting rate by 30%. At present, more than 60 furniture companies prepare materials at the center.

Reporters learned that after the Longhui center matures, 16 other furniture clusters in Nankang will build new shared preparation centers step by step. They will support the Nankang furniture industry with personalized custom orders, large-scale production, and smart services.
