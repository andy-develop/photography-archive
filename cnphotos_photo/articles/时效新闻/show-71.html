Li Jieyi: 70 Glorious Years, Marching into a New Liangshan

Published: 2022-12-28 | Category: News

This year marks the 70th anniversary of the Liangshan Yi Autonomous Prefecture in Sichuan Province. Over the past 70 years, under the care of the Party Central Committee and the State Council, and under the bright light of the Party's ethnic policies, the people of all ethnic groups in Liangshan have marched with pride onto the broad road of socialism. They have made a historic leap of "crossing a thousand years in one step." 

On November 5, a celebration marking the 70th anniversary of the Liangshan Yi Autonomous Prefecture was held in Xichang. People from all walks of life and people of all ethnic groups from the 17 counties and cities of Liangshan came together to celebrate the anniversary. 

Since the new era began, the people of Liangshan have followed Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era. They keep in mind the earnest instructions that General Secretary Xi Jinping gave when he visited Liangshan. They have united, striven, and fought hard. They won the fight against poverty. Together with the people of the whole country, they built a moderately prosperous society in all respects. They made another historic leap. The economy of Liangshan has grown quickly. Work on ethnic unity and progress has moved forward steadily. The level of social civility has risen greatly. The face of cities and countryside has changed beyond recognition. 

The Liangshan Yi Autonomous Prefecture was founded in 1952. It carried out democratic reform in 1956. It made the thousand-year leap from a slave society to a socialist society. Liangshan is the largest area of Yi settlement in the country. It is also the area in Sichuan with the most ethnic groups and the largest minority population.
