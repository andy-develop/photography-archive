Tacheng, Xinjiang: Immigration Police Bring the Law to People During National Cybersecurity Awareness Week

Published: 2022-09-07 | Category: News | Source:https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-51.html

On September 6, 2022, the Laoba Border Police Station of the Tacheng Border Management Detachment in Xinjiang sent officers to local squares and farm fields. They told the public about cybersecurity. The officers hung banners, handed out flyers, answered questions on the spot, and talked and interacted with people. They spread cybersecurity knowledge to workers and residents. The work raised everyone's cybersecurity awareness. It called on people to pay attention to cybersecurity, protect their data, and safeguard their personal information. It also warned people about telecom and online fraud. It built a strong atmosphere in which everyone takes part, and it helped build a joint line of cybersecurity defense.
