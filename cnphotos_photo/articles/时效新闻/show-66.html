Ili, Xinjiang: Snow Patrol at a Snowy Border

Published: 2022-10-31 | Category: News | Original: https://www.cnphotos.net/photo/show-66.html

        On October 30, 2022, heavy snow fell suddenly in border mountain areas of Ili, Xinjiang, after a cold air mass. The temperature dropped below zero. Police officers and auxiliary officers at the Qiete Aksu border police post braved the severe cold. The post is under the Yichegashan border police station, Khorgos border management brigade, Ili border management detachment. They climbed over icy passes, waded across frozen rivers, and crossed steep peaks to patrol the border on snow. They patrolled a border line more than 2,800 meters above sea level. They carefully checked for all kinds of hidden risks. They strengthened the border security barrier to keep the border safe and stable.

(Correspondents: Wang Pengfei, Xuan Xuangang)
